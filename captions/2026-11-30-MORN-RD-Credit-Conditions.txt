**Five conditions to check before claiming the UAE R&D credit.**

The credit can be worth up to 50% of qualifying spend, but the conditions are strict.

**The checklist:**
▶ **Pre-approval:** the project must be approved by the Emirates Research and Development Council, and you must meet its ongoing requirements.
▶ **Location:** only R&D activities carried out in the UAE qualify.
▶ **Funding:** you must bear the cost. Spend funded by federal or local government grants does not qualify.
▶ **Size:** at least AED 500,000 of qualifying spend per project in the tax period.
▶ **Clawback:** if the business leaves the Corporate Tax net or becomes a Qualifying Free Zone Person within five years, the credit must be repaid.

Unused credits can be carried forward if ownership and business continuity conditions are met, and transferred within groups with 75% or more common ownership.

The Council's application process and timelines are still being confirmed, so watch for updates.

✅ Keep project plans, technical goals and time records from day one.

✅ Check your grant funding before you count the credit in your budget.
