6 employee benefits where you can now recover VAT.

FTA Decision No. 17 of 2026 took effect on 1 October.

It covers goods and services you give employees free of charge.

You need a contract or documented policy, and every condition for that case must be met.

1. Transport: home-to-work, client sites or job duties, with no cash-allowance option.

2. Food and drinks: only where staff are at remote sites with no food facilities or restaurants nearby.

3. Accommodation: operationally required, near the work site, with no cash alternative.

4. New joiners' housing: temporary, up to 30 days.

5. Phones, data and home internet: needed for work, with a documented usage policy and reasonable monitoring.

6. Parking: business use only, a documented policy, and receipts showing date, time, amount and tax.

✅ Check your employment contracts first. A cash-allowance option can block recovery on transport and accommodation.

✅ Then line up your policies and receipts before your next VAT return.
