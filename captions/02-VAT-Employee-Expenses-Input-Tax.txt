**6 employee benefits where you can now recover VAT.**

FTA Decision No. 17 of 2026 took effect on 1 October.

It covers goods and services you give employees free of charge.

You need a contract or documented policy, and every condition for that case must be met.

▶ **Transport:** home-to-work, client sites or job duties, with no cash-allowance option.
▶ **Food and drinks:** only where staff are at remote sites with no food facilities or restaurants nearby.
▶ **Accommodation:** operationally required, near the work site, with no cash alternative.
▶ **New joiners' housing:** temporary, up to 30 days.
▶ **Phones, data and home internet:** needed for work, with a documented usage policy and reasonable monitoring.
▶ **Parking:** business use only, a documented policy, and receipts showing date, time, amount and tax.

✅ Check your employment contracts first. A cash-allowance option can block recovery on transport and accommodation.

✅ Then line up your policies and receipts before your next VAT return.
