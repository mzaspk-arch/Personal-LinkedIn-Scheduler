**Choosing an e-invoicing provider is a long-term decision. Choose carefully.**

Every UAE business in scope must appoint an Accredited Service Provider (ASP). Businesses with revenue below AED 50 million have until 31 March 2027.

The cheapest quote is rarely the cheapest option.

**What to check:**
▶ **Experience:** live Peppol and e-invoicing projects, not only a UAE accreditation.
▶ **ERP fit:** a tested connector for your system, whether Odoo, SAP, Microsoft Dynamics or a local ERP, and who maintains it.
▶ **Security:** data protection certifications and clear terms on where data is stored.
▶ **Service:** response times, support hours and how rejected invoices are handled.
▶ **Commercials:** transparent pricing per invoice or per year, and what happens when volumes grow.

Ask for a live demo using your own invoice types, including credit notes and advance payments.

✅ Shortlist three ASPs and test each with ten real invoices.

✅ Agree in writing who fixes integration errors: the ASP, your ERP partner or your team.
