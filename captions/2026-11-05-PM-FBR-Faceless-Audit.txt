**FBR audits now run through your IRIS inbox.**

FBR S.R.O. 1828(I)/2026 brought the faceless audit and assessment rules (Chapter XX, Income Tax Rules 2002) into force from 15 October 2026.

Designated cases are now handled by the National Faceless Centre: audits under sections 177 and 214C, section 111 proceedings and assessments.

**What changes for you:**
▶ **DIN on every notice:** notices and orders are generated by FBR's system with a Digital Identification Number (rule 238).
▶ **Replies via IRIS only:** documents and evidence go through your own IRIS account (rule 239).
▶ **No single officer:** audit, assessment and quality control sit with different officers (rule 237).
▶ **E-hearings:** hearings are electronic, and physical verification needs separate authorisation (rule 241).

Without a face-to-face meeting, a missed deadline is more likely to end in an assessment based on the data FBR already holds.

✅ Log in to IRIS now and confirm your email and mobile number are current.

✅ Keep bank statements, invoices and certificates as searchable digital files, ready to upload.

Source: FBR S.R.O. 1828(I)/2026, 15 October 2026: https://www.fbr.gov.pk/ShowSROs?Department=Income+Tax
