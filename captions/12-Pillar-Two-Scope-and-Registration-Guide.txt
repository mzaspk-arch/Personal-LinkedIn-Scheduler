**Is your group in scope of UAE Top-up Tax?**

The FTA's guide TTGREG1 explains who must register for the Domestic Minimum Top-up Tax.

**Key points:**

▶ **In scope:** multinational groups with revenue of EUR 750 million or more in at least 2 of the 4 preceding Fiscal Years.
▶ It applies to Fiscal Years beginning on or after 1 January 2025.
▶ Purely domestic UAE groups are out of scope, whatever their revenue.
▶ You must register even if your Top-up Tax is zero, for example under a safe harbour.
▶ Late registration costs AED 10,000 per Entity.

Top-up Tax registration is separate from Corporate Tax registration.

✅ Fiscal Year ended before 30 April 2026? Your deadline is 30 November 2026.
