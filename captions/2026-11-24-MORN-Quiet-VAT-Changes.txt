**Three quieter VAT changes from 1 October can still cost you.**

Most attention went to the big FTA decisions. Cabinet Decision No. 149 of 2026 also amended the VAT Executive Regulation, and several changes apply from 1 October 2026.

**Three to check:**
▶ **Composite supplies:** where parts of a supply are, in substance, linked and inseparable, they are treated as one supply. Review how you split bundles on invoices.
▶ **Cash payments:** input VAT cannot be recovered on a supply above an amount to be set by Ministerial Decision, where it is paid or intended to be paid in cash. The amount has not been published yet.
▶ **Employee accommodation:** input VAT is generally not recoverable unless the accommodation is required by a Ministry of Human Resources and Emiratisation decision.

The apportionment change comes later, for tax years starting on or after 1 October 2027.

✅ Move regular supplier payments to bank transfer now, before the cash limit is set.

✅ Review bundled contracts and how each part is priced and taxed.

Source: Cabinet Decision No. 149 of 2026, Ministry of Finance (mof.gov.ae).
