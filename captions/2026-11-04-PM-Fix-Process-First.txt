**Automate a broken process? It just breaks faster.**

Most SME owners buy software to fix a problem.

But software doesn't fix a process. It copies it.

**Three reasons to fix the process first:**
▶ **It copies the gaps:** if approvals are unclear today, the new system will route them to the wrong person, only quicker.
▶ **Workarounds go underground:** when the system doesn't fit, staff go back to Excel and WhatsApp, and your "single source of truth" has holes.
▶ **Fixing is cheaper on paper:** changing a flowchart costs a meeting. Changing a configured system costs a consultant.

My order is simple: map it, fix it, then automate it.

Map the steps as they really happen, not as the policy says.

Remove the steps nobody needs.

Then choose the tool that fits the cleaned-up process.

✅ Pick one process this week, such as purchase approvals, and draw it on one page.

✅ Mark every step where someone re-types data or waits for a reply. Those are your automation candidates.

Agree or disagree? Which process in your business would you fix before automating?
