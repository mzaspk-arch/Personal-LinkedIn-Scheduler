**Your ERP will fail on dirty data, not on software.**

Most ERP projects do not struggle because of the software.

They struggle because the old data was moved across as it was.

**Three reasons:**
▶ **Duplicate customers and suppliers:** the same client under three names breaks every sales and ageing report from day one.
▶ **Wrong opening balances:** if the first trial balance does not agree, nobody trusts the numbers again.
▶ **No owner:** clean-up is everyone's job, so it becomes nobody's job.

A new system makes bad data travel faster. It does not fix it.

Before go-live, one named person should sign off customers, suppliers, items, the chart of accounts and opening balances.

✅ Run a duplicate check on your customer and supplier lists this month.

✅ Agree opening balances to signed accounts before you migrate.
