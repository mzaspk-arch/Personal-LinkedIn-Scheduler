**Overseas Pakistani? Your NRVA may not need a tax return.**

Many overseas Pakistanis believe any bank account in Pakistan means filing a return every year.

Finance Act 2026 changed that for people who keep their money in foreign currency and non-resident rupee accounts (FCVA, FCBVA, NRVA, NRVBA).

**No return or registration is needed if your only Pakistan-source income is:**
▶ **Profit** on balances in these accounts.
▶ **Profit on government securities** bought through these accounts.
▶ **Capital gains or dividends** on PSX shares or mutual funds bought through them, or gains on property bought through an FCVA or NRVA.

Earn anything else in Pakistan, such as rent, salary or business income? The normal filing rules still apply.

✅ List every source of Pakistan income you had this tax year.

✅ Check that your investments were paid for through these accounts, and keep the bank records.

Source: FBR Circular No. 02 of 2026-27, 8 September 2026: https://download1.fbr.gov.pk/Docs/202698139527880CircularNo.2of2026-27.pdf
