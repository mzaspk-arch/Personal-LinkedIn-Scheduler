**Every rupee comes from one of five capitals.**

Lesson 08 from Gharebon Ka MBA with Dr. Zeeshan-ul-Hassan Usmani.

**The five capitals a person can own:**
▶ **Body:** two arms, two legs and the hours in a day. Everyone starts here.
▶ **Skill:** something specific you do better or faster than others.
▶ **Proof:** evidence you've done it, such as results, references and a name people repeat.
▶ **Network:** people who would take your call, enough to hire, refer or open a door.
▶ **Capital:** money, tools or ownership that earn while you sleep.

Most people run on the first one and were never told the other four existed.

Everyone starts with Body. Nobody should end there.

The exercise was simple: one honest sentence for each capital. An empty line is information, and often the most useful line on the page.

✅ Write the five words down and add one honest sentence beside each.

✅ Circle the emptiest line. That's where the next 90 days should go.

Which capital is strongest for you, and which is empty?
