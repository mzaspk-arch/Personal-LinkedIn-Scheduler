**Most people fix a Show gap by studying more.**

Lesson 17 from Gharebon Ka MBA with Dr. Zeeshan-ul-Hassan Usmani.

When an attempt to get paid stalls, the cause is usually one of five kinds of missing.

**Five kinds of missing:**
▶ **Know:** you haven't learned it yet.
▶ **Do:** learned, but slow. Only repetition fixes it.
▶ **Show:** good work, but nothing a stranger can open and judge.
▶ **Reach:** excellent, and unheard of.
▶ **Fit:** it works, and it hollows you out.

Each is cured by a different action. Treating the wrong one is how years disappear while a person feels busy.

Diagnose by feeling, and you'll land on Know every time, because studying is the most comfortable fix.

**The order that pays soonest:**
▶ Settle Fit, then build Show, then open Reach, and run learning underneath.

✅ Take your last real attempt to get paid and mark where it stopped.

✅ Name the gap, and pick the action that matches it.

Which kind of missing is yours right now?
