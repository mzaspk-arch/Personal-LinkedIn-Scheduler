**Downward transfer pricing adjustments must always be disclosed.**

Related-party and connected-person transactions must be at arm's length. If you charged a related party more than arm's length, you may be able to reduce taxable income with a downward adjustment in your Corporate Tax return.

FTA Public Clarification CTP011 explains how.

**Key points:**
▶ **No prior approval:** you make the adjustment yourself in the return.
▶ **Always disclose:** every downward adjustment goes in the transfer pricing disclosure form. The usual disclosure thresholds do not apply.
▶ **Evidence:** keep the commercial reason the original price was not arm's length, a reconciliation from the accounts to the return, and a benchmarking analysis.
▶ **Symmetry:** expect the FTA to look for a matching adjustment by the related party.

Because downward adjustments reduce tax, they will be looked at closely.

✅ Review related-party pricing during the year, so fewer adjustments are needed.

✅ Prepare the evidence file with the return, not after a query.
