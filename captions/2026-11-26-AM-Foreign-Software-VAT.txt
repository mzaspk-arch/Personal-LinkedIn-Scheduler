**Foreign software still carries UAE VAT.**

Your CRM, cloud storage or design tool is billed from abroad with no VAT on the invoice.

That does not mean there is no VAT.

Under Article 48 of the VAT Decree-Law, a business that imports services for its business is treated as making a supply to itself. This is the reverse charge.

**What it means in practice:**
▶ **You account for the VAT:** at the rate that would apply if a UAE supplier had made the supply.
▶ **Timing:** declare it in the return for the tax period of the date of supply.
▶ **Records:** keep the supplier's invoice showing the details and the amount paid.
▶ **Since 1 January 2026:** you no longer issue a tax invoice to yourself (Federal Decree-Law No. 16 of 2025).

If your business can recover input tax under the normal rules, this VAT is generally recoverable too. If not, it becomes a real cost.

✅ List every overseas subscription paid by company card or bank transfer.

✅ Check each one appears as reverse charge in your VAT return.

Source: VAT Decree-Law and Executive Regulation, Article 48 (tax.gov.ae).
