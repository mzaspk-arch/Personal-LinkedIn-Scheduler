**You cannot price what you have never counted.**

Lesson 07 from Gharebon Ka MBA with Dr. Zeeshan-ul-Hassan Usmani.

Everyone gets asked what they do. Almost nobody gets asked what they own.

**Three questions most people can't answer:**
▶ **Your real monthly income:** everything that arrived last month, from every source. Not the salary on paper.
▶ **The hours it took:** commute, overtime, work taken home, the calls on Sunday. All of it.
▶ **What someone pays you for:** not what you enjoy or studied. What money has actually changed hands for.

If all three are blank, that's not a failure. It's the starting point.

Finance teams would never price a product without knowing its cost. Yet most of us price ourselves that way.

✅ Answer the three questions on paper tonight, using last month only.

✅ Keep the page. You'll need it for the next lesson: your real hourly rate.

Which of the three was hardest for you to answer?
