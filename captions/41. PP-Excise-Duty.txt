**Nicotine patches and gum are not taxed as tobacco in the UAE.**

Many importers and pharmacies still treat every Chapter 24 product the same way.

For Excise Tax, "tobacco and tobacco products" means anything under Chapter 24 of the GCC Common Customs Tariff, including electrically-heated cigarettes.

**Products covered include:**
▶ Cigarettes, cigars and cigarillos
▶ Chewing tobacco, snuff and hand rolling tobacco
▶ Cigarette rag, expanded tobacco and reconstituted tobacco sheets
▶ Herbal smoking products

**The exception, effective 1 October 2025:**
Chapter 24 products made exclusively to help people stop smoking are not tobacco products for Excise Tax.

▶ **HS 2404 91:** nicotine chewing gum, tablets and other forms
▶ **HS 2404 92:** patches and other forms
▶ **HS 2404 99:** sprays, nose drops and injections

The legal basis is Article 3 of Cabinet Decision No. 197 of 2025, read with Ministerial Decision No. 249 of 2025.

✅ Check the full 10-digit HS code on every cessation product you import or stock.

✅ Make sure the product is exclusively for cessation, and keep the supporting documents.
