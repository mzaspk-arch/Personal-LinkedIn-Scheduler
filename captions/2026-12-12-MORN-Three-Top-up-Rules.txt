**Under Pillar Two, low-taxed profit gets taxed somewhere.**

The global minimum tax has three charging rules. They work in a fixed order.

**1. Domestic minimum top-up tax (DMTT)**
The country where the profit is earned collects the top-up first. The UAE has one, so Top-up Tax on UAE profit is paid in the UAE.

**2. Income Inclusion Rule (IIR)**
If a country has no domestic top-up, the country of the parent company can collect it. A qualifying DMTT generally takes priority.

**3. Undertaxed Profits Rule (UTPR)**
If neither applies, other countries where the group operates can collect the top-up, even from entities with no link to the low-taxed profit.

The UAE has a DMTT but no IIR. So a UAE-parented group with low-taxed subsidiaries abroad may face top-up tax collected by other countries.

That is why the UAE chose to collect first: if the UAE does not, someone else will.
