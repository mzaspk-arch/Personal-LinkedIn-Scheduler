**E-invoicing fines are now capped, but they still add up.**

At the end of October 2026 the Ministry of Finance announced revised e-invoicing penalties, replacing Cabinet Decision No. 106 of 2025.

**What changed:**
▶ **Implementation breaches** by an issuer or recipient: AED 5,000 for the first violation, plus AED 5,000 for each month or part of a month it continues.
▶ **Cap:** a maximum of AED 50,000 for these breaches in any 12 consecutive months.
▶ **System failures:** the penalty for not notifying the FTA of a system failure has been removed.
▶ **No double penalties:** where the same person is issuer and recipient for the same violation, only one penalty applies.

Separate penalties can still apply to e-invoices and credit notes not issued and transmitted on time.

A cap is not a budget. Ten months of delay still costs AED 50,000.

✅ Check your go-live date and plan to be ready a month before it.

✅ Keep a log of system outages and how you recovered, even though notification is no longer penalised.
