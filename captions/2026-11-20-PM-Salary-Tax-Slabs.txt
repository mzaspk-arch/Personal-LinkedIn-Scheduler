**Salaried? Check your payslip against the new tax slabs.**

Finance Act 2026 cut income tax rates for salaried people in Pakistan.

If your employer's payroll hasn't been updated, you may be paying more tax than you need to each month.

**The first three slabs:**
▶ **Up to Rs 600,000 a year:** 0%.
▶ **Rs 600,000 to Rs 1.2 million:** 1% of the amount above Rs 600,000.
▶ **Rs 1.2 million to Rs 2.2 million:** Rs 6,000 plus 11% of the amount above Rs 1.2 million.

**Higher earners:** the 9% surcharge on salaried people with taxable income above Rs 10 million has been withdrawn. The 10% surcharge on other individuals and AOPs continues.

The full table goes up to 35% on income above Rs 7 million.

✅ Compare the tax on your latest payslip with the new slab for your annual salary.

✅ If it doesn't match, ask HR or payroll which table they are using.

Source: FBR Circular No. 02 of 2026-27, 8 September 2026: https://download1.fbr.gov.pk/Docs/202698139527880CircularNo.2of2026-27.pdf
