**Vending machine sales no longer need a VAT tax invoice.**

The FTA issued VAT Directive No. 8 of 2026 on 19 November 2026.

A machine can't print a tax invoice for every can or snack. Now it doesn't have to, as long as your records hold up.

**Keep, as a minimum, for each supply:**
▶ **What:** a description of the goods or services
▶ **How much:** total consideration received and VAT charged
▶ **When:** the date of supply under Article 26(2) of the VAT Law

**What counts as a vending machine:**
An automated self-service device that supplies goods or services immediately when the customer uses it, with no human input from the supplier.
Restocking, monitoring or maintenance does not count as human input.

**If a third party operates your machines:**
Payment is treated as collected on the earlier of the date the operator confirms the amount collected, or the day after 30 days from the operator's collection.

✅ Check that your machine sales data captures all three record fields.

✅ Ask operators to report collections well inside 30 days.

Source: https://tax.gov.ae/Datafolder/Files/Pdf/2026/legislation/2026-08%20-VAT-Vending-Machines.pdf
