**AED 100 million: the threshold for UAE transfer pricing certainty.**

Related-party pricing is one of the most common audit questions under UAE Corporate Tax.

An Advance Pricing Agreement (APA) lets you agree the arm's length pricing with the FTA before the tax periods begin.

**Key points from the FTA's APA guide:**
▶ **Who:** businesses with controlled transactions with related parties.
▶ **Threshold:** AED 100 million per tax period. It is indicative, and safe-harbour transactions are excluded.
▶ **Term:** 3 to 5 tax periods, future periods only.
▶ **Fees:** AED 30,000 to apply, AED 15,000 to renew. Both non-refundable.
▶ **Process:** pre-filing consultation (6–9 months), application, evaluation and negotiation, then finalisation.
▶ **Phasing:** domestic unilateral APAs opened on 30 December 2025. Cross-border ones follow once the FTA announces the start date.

Signing is not the end of the work.

A breach of a critical assumption must be reported within 20 business days, and an annual APA declaration is mandatory.
