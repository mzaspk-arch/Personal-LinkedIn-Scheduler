"We're in a Free Zone, so we're covered." Not necessarily.

Public Clarification TAXP010 shows Corporate Tax, VAT and Excise Tax each define their zones differently.

Key points:

1. A VAT Designated Zone isn't automatically a Designated Zone for Corporate Tax.

2. A Corporate Tax Designated Zone must be both a VAT Designated Zone and a Corporate Tax Free Zone.

3. The 0% rate on qualifying distribution income applies only in or from a Corporate Tax Designated Zone.

4. An Excise Designated Zone needs security controls, customs supervision and a Warehouse Keeper.

5. A Qualifying Free Zone Person that breaches the conditions loses its status for that period and the next four.

✅ Check your zone's status separately for each tax.

✅ Remember: 0% applies only to Qualifying Income.
