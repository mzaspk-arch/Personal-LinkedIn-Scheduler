**Old tax credits with the FTA? Claim by 31 Dec.**

Since 1 January 2026, a credit balance with the FTA has a time limit.

Under Federal Decree-Law No. 17 of 2025, you must ask for a refund, or use the balance against tax due, within 5 years of the end of the tax period it relates to.

Older balances get one last chance.

**The one-year window:**
▶ **Who:** businesses whose 5-year period ended before 1 January 2026, or ends during 2026.
▶ **Deadline:** submit the refund request by 31 December 2026.
▶ **Errors found later:** you can file a voluntary disclosure on that request within 2 years of submitting it, if the FTA has not decided yet.

After the deadline, the right to recover these amounts can be lost for good.

✅ Prepare refund requests for the oldest balances first, with invoices and returns to support them.
