**A Free Zone company with no staff can lose 0%.**

To be a Qualifying Free Zone Person, a company must keep adequate substance in the Free Zone, among other conditions. The FTA's summary of private clarifications shows how strictly this is read.

**Lessons from the clarifications:**
▶ **No automatic pass:** passive or asset-based activities, such as leasing property to related parties, still need substance.
▶ **People:** a property lessor with no full-time employees may fail where nobody is dedicated to the core work: contracts, renewals, compliance and enforcing rights.
▶ **Related-party staff:** employees sponsored by another group company count only where the Free Zone company bears the cost and controls their work.
▶ **Shared offices:** can work if the space is enough for the activity.

Also remember the de minimis rule: non-qualifying revenue must not exceed AED 5 million or 5% of total revenue, whichever is lower.

Failing the conditions means losing QFZP status for that tax period and the next four.

✅ Write down who performs each core activity, and where.

✅ Keep payroll recharges, job descriptions and office agreements on file.
