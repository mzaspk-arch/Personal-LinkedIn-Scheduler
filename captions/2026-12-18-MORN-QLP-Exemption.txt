**Investment limited partnerships can be exempt from UAE Corporate Tax.**

Cabinet Decision No. 34 of 2025 introduced an exemption for Qualifying Limited Partnerships (QLPs), on application to the FTA.

**The conditions:**
▶ **Main activity:** investment business. Any other activity must be ancillary or incidental, with revenue of no more than 5% of total revenue for the year.
▶ **No UAE property income:** the QLP must not earn income from UAE immovable property.
▶ **Purpose:** avoiding Corporate Tax must not be its main purpose.
▶ **Subsidiaries:** UAE entities wholly owned by a QLP may also be exempt in certain cases, again with no UAE property income.

**How investors are taxed:**
▶ The exempt QLP is effectively transparent.
▶ Investors subject to Corporate Tax include their share of the QLP's net income, based on their ownership.
▶ Distributions they receive from the QLP are excluded, so the same profit is not taxed twice.

For family offices and private investment vehicles, this can simplify the structure considerably.

✅ Check whether your investment vehicle's income mix stays within the 5% limit.

✅ Make sure investors can report their share of the QLP's income each year.
