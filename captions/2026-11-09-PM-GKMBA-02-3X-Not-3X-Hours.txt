**Nobody triples an income by tripling hours.**

Lesson 02 from Gharebon Ka MBA with Dr. Zeeshan-ul-Hassan Usmani.

The programme's target is blunt: three times whatever you earn now.

On a modest salary or a seven-figure one, the target is the same multiple.

**Why hours can't get you there:**
▶ **The week is fixed:** there are only 168 hours in it.
▶ **The road ends badly:** stacking hours leads to burnout, not wealth.
▶ **What changes instead:** the market you serve and the leverage you use.

In finance we see it every month: the people who grow fastest rarely work the longest hours.

They change who pays them, and what does the work.

✅ Write down your current monthly number. That is your baseline.

✅ Then write the 3X figure next to it, and keep the page.

Agree or disagree: can a bigger income ever come from more hours?
