**The market pays for scarcity, not difficulty.**

Lesson 12 from Gharebon Ka MBA with Dr. Zeeshan-ul-Hassan Usmani.

After filtering your list, rank what survives.

**Score each skill from 1 to 5 on:**
▶ **Demand:** are people already spending money on this, this month, near you or online?
▶ **Proof:** can you show a stranger evidence within ten minutes?
▶ **Rarity:** how few people around you do it equally well?

Multiply the three. The highest number is where you start, not the skill you love most.

The worked example in class made the point: a hard skill with low demand scores below an easier one people urgently want.

The market doesn't pay for difficulty. It pays for scarcity that somebody urgently needs.

If your top three all score low on demand, that's not defeat. It means adding one adjacent skill the market already buys.

✅ Score your top skills on Demand, Proof and Rarity, and multiply.

✅ Write the winner on a fresh page. That's your engine for the next twelve weeks.
