**Four income engines. Pick one.**

Lesson 05 from Gharebon Ka MBA with Dr. Zeeshan-ul-Hassan Usmani.

Every way to grow income runs on one of four engines.

**The four engines:**
▶ **Career:** a promotion, a move, a renegotiated salary.
▶ **Client:** freelance work, consulting, advisory, services.
▶ **Product:** courses, tools, software, intellectual property.
▶ **Capital:** ownership, reinvestment, partnership, investing.

The warning that stayed with me: picking all four is the most common way to spend twelve weeks and end exactly where you started.

Focus is the strategy. One engine, run properly, beats four engines started halfway.

For a finance professional, each engine looks very different: a CFO role, an advisory practice, a software product, or equity in a business.

✅ Write down the one engine you'll run for the next 90 days.

✅ Park the other three on a "later" list, so they stop pulling at your attention.

Which engine are you running right now?
