**AED 50m+ revenue? E-invoicing goes live on 1 January 2027.**

On 25 October 2026 the FTA reminded businesses of the e-invoicing deadlines.

If your annual revenue is AED 50 million or more, you had to appoint an Accredited Service Provider (ASP) by 30 October 2026.

The system itself must be running by 1 January 2027. That is less than eight weeks away.

**What to do now:**
▶ **No ASP yet:** appoint one now. The penalty is AED 5,000 for each month, or part of a month, of delay, and that includes not appointing an ASP on time.
▶ **Integrate:** contract with the ASP and connect it to your ERP or invoicing system.
▶ **EmaraTax:** complete the e-invoicing steps on the FTA's platform.
▶ **Under AED 50m:** your dates are 31 March 2027 for the ASP and 1 July 2027 to go live.

✅ Test real invoices from your ERP through the ASP before December, including credit notes.

✅ Clean customer and supplier master data first. Every gap shows up on every invoice.
