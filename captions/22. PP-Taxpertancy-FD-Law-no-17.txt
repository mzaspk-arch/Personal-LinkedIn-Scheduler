**Old VAT credits on your account? They now expire after 5 years.**

Federal Decree-Law No. 17 of 2025 amended the UAE Tax Procedures Law from 1 January 2026.

Many businesses still carry VAT credit balances from years ago.

Under the amended law, those balances no longer sit there forever.

**What changed:**
▶ **Five-year limit:** refunds, offsets and FTA credit applications must be made within five years from the end of the relevant tax period.
▶ **Refund procedures:** clearer steps and timelines for refund requests where a credit balance exists.
▶ **Audit and admin rules:** updated audit powers, voluntary disclosure rules and statutory timelines across VAT, Corporate Tax and Excise Tax.

In plain terms: an unclaimed credit is now a deadline, not a savings account.

For older credits, a one-year transition window under the amendments ends on 31 December 2026.

✅ Pull your EmaraTax credit balance and list it by tax period.

✅ Apply for a refund or offset on the oldest periods first, before the year ends.
