**Want certainty on transfer pricing? Look at the unilateral APA route.**

The FTA's updated policy on clarifications took effect on 1 March 2026.

**On Unilateral Advance Pricing Agreements:**

▶ They currently cover domestic Related Party transactions. Cross-border applications are expected from Q4 2026.
▶ **Threshold:** expected transactions of at least AED 100 million per Tax Period.
▶ Each agreement covers 3 to 5 Tax Periods.
▶ The FTA aims to conclude within 30 months of a complete application.
▶ Administrative exceptions are valid for 3 years.

Because you apply well before the first covered period, early planning matters.

✅ Groups with large domestic related-party flows: map them against the AED 100m threshold.

✅ Start pre-filing talks early.
