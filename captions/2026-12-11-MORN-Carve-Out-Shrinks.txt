**Your Pillar Two carve-out gets smaller every year until 2033.**

The substance-based income exclusion reduces the profit that Top-up Tax applies to. It is a percentage of eligible payroll plus a percentage of eligible tangible assets in each country.

During the transition, both percentages fall each year:

▶ **2025:** 9.6% of payroll and 7.6% of tangible assets.
▶ **2026:** 9.4% and 7.4%.
▶ **2027:** 9.2% and 7.2%.
▶ **2028:** 9.0% and 7.0%.
▶ **2033 onwards:** 5% and 5%.

The rate depends on when the fiscal year starts.

**What it means:** with the same staff and the same assets, the carve-out shrinks and the Top-up Tax rises, even if profit stays flat. For a UAE entity with AED 40 million of payroll, the payroll carve-out alone falls from AED 3.84 million in 2025 to AED 2 million from 2033.

Real substance in the UAE still helps. It just helps less each year.
