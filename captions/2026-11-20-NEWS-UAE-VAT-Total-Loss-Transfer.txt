**Total loss? Handing the wreck to your insurer isn't a VAT supply.**

The FTA issued VAT Directive No. 6 of 2026 on 18 November 2026.

It settles a question that comes up in vehicle, equipment and property claims: does VAT apply when the insurer takes the damaged asset?

The answer is no, as long as these conditions are met.

**When the transfer is outside VAT:**
▶ **Total loss:** the asset cannot be economically or safely repaired.
▶ **Part of the settlement:** ownership passes from the insured to the insurer under the claim.
▶ **No separate price:** nothing is paid for the asset beyond the insurance compensation.

The compensation itself, and the value the insurer puts on the asset to work out the loss, are not treated as payment for the asset.

Deregistering the asset with the authority does not change this.

**What happens next:** if the insurer later sells the wreck, that sale is the insurer's own supply under the normal VAT rules.

✅ Keep the total-loss assessment and claim settlement documents. The directive requires both sides to retain them.

✅ If a claim pays you anything extra for the wreck, review the VAT treatment before you account for it.
