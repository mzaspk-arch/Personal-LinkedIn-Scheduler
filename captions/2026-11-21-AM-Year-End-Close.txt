**31 December year-end? Start your close now.**

Six weeks before year-end is when a smooth close is decided.

Leave it to January and your team rushes, your auditor waits and errors reach your financial statements and your Corporate Tax return.

**Four things to start this month:**
▶ **Stock count:** fix the date, the team and the cut-off rules.
▶ **Receivables:** chase overdue balances now, while there is still time to collect before year-end.
▶ **Reconcile:** bank accounts, VAT accounts and intercompany balances, so differences surface early.
▶ **Auditor:** agree the timetable and the list of documents they will ask for.

For a 31 December 2026 year-end, the Corporate Tax return is due by 30 September 2027. A clean close makes that return much easier.

✅ Put the stock count date and the auditor's fieldwork dates in the calendar this week.

✅ Assign one owner for each reconciliation, with a deadline in mid-December.
