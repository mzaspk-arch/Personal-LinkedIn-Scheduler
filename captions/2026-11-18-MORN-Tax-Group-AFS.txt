**Consolidated IFRS accounts are not the same as Tax Group accounts.**

A UAE Tax Group files one Corporate Tax return based on Aggregated Financial Statements (AFS). For tax periods starting on or after 1 January 2025, every Tax Group needs audited special-purpose AFS.

**How AFS differ from consolidation:**
▶ **Members only:** only UAE resident Tax Group members are included. Foreign subsidiaries are left out.
▶ **No acquisition accounting:** no goodwill, bargain-purchase gains or IFRS 3 fair-value uplifts.
▶ **No NCI:** members' equity is simply aggregated.
▶ **Investments stay:** the parent's investment is not eliminated against the subsidiary's equity, and impairments stay in.
▶ **Currency:** presented in AED.

Transactions between members are still eliminated.

To form a Tax Group, members need at least 95% common ownership, the same financial year and the same accounting standards. None can be an Exempt Person or a Qualifying Free Zone Person.

✅ Build the aggregation working paper alongside your consolidation, not after it.
