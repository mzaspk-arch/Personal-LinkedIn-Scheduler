**Exempt from Corporate Tax? You still have to apply.**

FTA Decision No. 15 of 2026 replaced Decision No. 7 of 2023 from 15 September 2026.

**The new timelines:**

▶ Register for Corporate Tax first, then apply for exemption.
▶ **General rule:** apply within 90 Business Days after the end of the Tax Period.
▶ Persons covered by Cabinet Decision No. 55 of 2025: apply by 31 December 2026.
▶ Certain wholly owned subsidiaries, for Tax Periods ended before 1 January 2026: apply by 31 October 2026.
▶ An approved exemption applies from the start of the Tax Period in the application.

Subsidiaries apply only after their parent has applied.
