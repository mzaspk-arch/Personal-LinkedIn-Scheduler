Exempt from Corporate Tax? You still have to apply.

FTA Decision No. 15 of 2026 replaced Decision No. 7 of 2023 from 15 September 2026.

The new timelines:

1. Register for Corporate Tax first, then apply for exemption.

2. General rule: apply within 90 Business Days after the end of the Tax Period.

3. Persons covered by Cabinet Decision No. 55 of 2025: apply by 31 December 2026.

4. Certain wholly owned subsidiaries, for Tax Periods ended before 1 January 2026: apply by 31 October 2026.

5. An approved exemption applies from the start of the Tax Period in the application.

Subsidiaries apply only after their parent has applied.
