**Oman just put deadlines and penalties into its Pillar Two law.**

Royal Decree No. 101/2026 was issued on 29 October and published in the Official Gazette (Issue 1672) on 1 November 2026.

It amends Oman's top-up tax law for members of multinational groups with revenue of €750 million or more in at least two of the four previous years.

**What in-scope entities in Oman must now do:**
▶ **Register:** within 120 days from the date the entity becomes subject to the tax.
▶ **Appoint:** a designated reporting entity at registration.
▶ **File and pay:** the top-up tax return and the GloBE information return within 15 months after the financial year ends.
▶ **Late payment:** 1% of the unpaid tax for each month or part of a month.
▶ **Corrections:** an amended return within 30 days of finding an error.
▶ **Records:** kept for 10 years, including by excluded entities.
▶ **Exemptions:** tax exemptions under other laws do not cover top-up tax unless they say so.

Administrative fines can reach 25% of the top-up tax due or OMR 10,000, whichever is higher.

The core rules apply from the tax year starting 1 January 2025.
