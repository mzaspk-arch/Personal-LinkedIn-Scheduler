**A skill is sellable only if someone pays for it.**

Lesson 11 from Gharebon Ka MBA with Dr. Zeeshan-ul-Hassan Usmani.

One test only: somebody, somewhere, already pays for this. Not could. Does.

**What doesn't make a skill sellable:**
▶ **Talent:** nobody pays for potential. They pay for a task finished to a standard.
▶ **A degree:** it proves attendance. The market asks what you can do on Monday.
▶ **Passion:** enjoying something is a fine reason to keep it, not a reason to price it.

A driver, an accountant and a data scientist all exchange a defined competence for money. The rate differs. The mechanism doesn't.

The exercise: list twenty sellable skills, not three. Spread across hands-on, desk and trust-based work.

One skill is a job you can lose. Twenty is insurance.

✅ Write twenty skills, without judging any of them until the list is done.

✅ Then ask of each one: has money ever changed hands for this?

How many of your skills would pass the "someone pays" test?
