**Your salary is not your rate. Do the division.**

Lesson 10 from Gharebon Ka MBA with Dr. Zeeshan-ul-Hassan Usmani.

This one is pure arithmetic, and it's uncomfortable.

**The honest ledger:**
▶ **Every rupee that landed last month:** salary, freelance, commission, rent, everything.
▶ **Every hour it took:** working hours, commute, unpaid overtime, weekend calls, the 2 a.m. thinking.
▶ **Divide the first by the second:** that's your true hourly rate.

Not your salary. Not your hope.

According to the course, most people find their real rate is between half and two-thirds of what they believed.

Use one month only. It's enough, and it stops you averaging away a bad month.

As a CFO, I'd call this unit economics for a person. You can't improve a margin you've never calculated.

✅ Take last month's total income and total hours, and divide.

✅ Write the answer down and circle it. Nobody sees it but you.

When did you last calculate your real hourly rate?
