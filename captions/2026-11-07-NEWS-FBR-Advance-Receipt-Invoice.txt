**Took a customer advance? Sales tax is due that month.**

FBR issued S.R.O. 2063(I)/2026 on 5 November 2026, substituting Rule 160 of the Sales Tax Rules, 2006.

It covers registered persons who receive advance payments against a supply, in full, in part or in instalments.

**What changes:**
▶ **Each advance:** issue an advance receipt invoice at the time you receive it.
▶ **Output tax:** declare it in the return for the tax period in which the advance is received.
▶ **Instalments:** a separate invoice for each payment, electronically linked to the earlier ones.
▶ **Final invoice:** issued on supply, referring to the advance invoices. Tax already accounted for is not charged again.
▶ **Rate:** the rate at receipt applies to the advance; the rate at supply applies to the balance.
▶ **Changes or cancellations:** adjust under section 9 and Rules 19 to 22, with linked debit and credit notes.

This turns a cash event into a tax event.
