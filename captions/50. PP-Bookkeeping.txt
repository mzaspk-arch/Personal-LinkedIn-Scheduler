**Most cash surprises start with books updated once a quarter.**

Bookkeeping is not paperwork. It is the data every decision, bank loan and tax return depends on.

**Five routines that keep the books clean:**
▶ **Daily:** record every sale, purchase and payment the day it happens.
▶ **Weekly:** match income and expenses to receipts and invoices.
▶ **Monthly:** reconcile every bank and card account to its statement.
▶ **Monthly:** produce a profit and loss and a balance sheet, and actually read them.
▶ **Yearly:** close the books and prepare statements your auditor and tax advisor can rely on.

The bank reconciliation is the step most often skipped.

It is also the one that catches duplicate payments, missing receipts and errors while they are still easy to fix.
