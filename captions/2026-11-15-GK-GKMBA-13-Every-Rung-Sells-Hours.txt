**Every rung so far still sells hours.**

Lesson 13 from Gharebon Ka MBA with Dr. Zeeshan-ul-Hassan Usmani.

This was the uncomfortable slide.

The driver sells hours. The accountant sells hours. The data scientist on a big salary sells hours too, just better-paid ones.

**What climbing really does:**
▶ **It raises the price of an hour.**
▶ **It never removes the ceiling,** because nobody has found a way to buy a 25th hour.
▶ **The only real break:** something you own does the earning, such as a product, a system, a team or a holding.

That's the moment money starts working instead of you.

It changed how I think about my own week. Some of the work should raise my rate. Some of it should build something that earns without me.

✅ Look at last week and mark which hours raised your rate, and which built an asset.

✅ Move one hour next week from the first column to the second.

Agree or disagree: is a high hourly rate still a ceiling?
