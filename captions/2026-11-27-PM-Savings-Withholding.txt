**How much tax comes off your savings profit?**

Before profit or dividends reach your account, tax is usually already deducted.

FBR's Withholding Tax Rate Card for tax year 2027 (sections 150 and 151) sets the rates.

**Rates if you are on the ATL:**
▶ **Bank deposit profit:** 20%.
▶ **Corporate sukuk, individuals and AOPs:** 10% where the return is under Rs 1 million, 12.5% above it.
▶ **Dividends, general case:** 15%.
▶ **Mutual funds earning 50% or more of income from profit on debt:** 25%.

If you are not on the Active Taxpayers' List, each of these rates doubles: 40%, 20% or 25%, 30% and 50%.

On Rs 1 million of bank profit, that is Rs 200,000 deducted on the ATL, or Rs 400,000 off it.

✅ Check your bank's tax deduction certificate against these rates.

✅ Make sure your name is on the ATL before your next profit payment.

Source: FBR Withholding Tax Rate Card, Tax Year 2027 (fbr.gov.pk).
