**Choosing an e-invoicing provider? Check these rules first.**

Ministerial Decision No. 64 of 2025, amended by Ministerial Decision No. 56 of 2026, sets who can be accredited.

**What a provider must show:**

▶ **New:** its e-invoicing product must have operated for at least 2 years.
▶ Active Peppol certification, with OpenPeppol conformance tests passed.
▶ Paid-up capital of AED 50,000, plus ISO 22301 and ISO/IEC 27001.
▶ **Insurance:** professional indemnity AED 2.5m, crime AED 5m, cyber fraud AED 5m.
▶ Accreditation lasts 2 years, with renewal at least 70 Business Days before expiry.

✅ Only work with providers accredited by the Ministry of Finance.

✅ Check how your ERP will connect to your provider before you sign.
