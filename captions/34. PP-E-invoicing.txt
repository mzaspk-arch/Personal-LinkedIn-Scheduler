**UAE e-invoicing goes live for large businesses in 2 months.**

From 1 January 2027, e-invoicing becomes mandatory for businesses with revenue of AED 50 million or more.

All other VAT-registered businesses follow on 1 July 2027.

The pilot phase for select businesses began on 1 July 2026.

**What changes:**
▶ **Format:** paper and PDF invoices are replaced by structured digital invoices.
▶ **Exchange:** e-invoices are issued and exchanged through Accredited Service Providers (ASPs).
▶ **Reporting:** invoice data is reported to the Federal Tax Authority (FTA).

The real work sits in your ERP or accounting system, not in the invoice template.

Customer and supplier data, tax codes and credit notes all need to be clean before go-live.

✅ Confirm which phase applies to you based on your revenue.

✅ Appoint your ASP early and test your ERP integration well before your date.
