**Treat e-invoicing as an IT project and it will struggle.**

The UAE uses a Peppol-based model with the PINT AE data standard. Invoices move through Accredited Service Providers, and the data goes to the FTA.

The software connection is the easy part.

**Where projects really struggle:**
▶ **Master data:** many ERPs do not capture every mandatory field, such as complete buyer details and line-level VAT. Fixing customer and supplier records takes months.
▶ **Process:** invoice approvals, advance payments, credit notes, self-billing and exception handling all need redesign.
▶ **Integration:** businesses with several systems may need middleware and two-way status updates.
▶ **Governance:** tax, finance, IT and the ASP need named owners and end-to-end testing.

Seen from both the finance side and the ERP side, the lesson is the same: the data, not the technology, sets the timeline.

✅ Start with a data-quality check on your customer and supplier master files.

✅ Map every invoice type you issue before you choose an ASP.
