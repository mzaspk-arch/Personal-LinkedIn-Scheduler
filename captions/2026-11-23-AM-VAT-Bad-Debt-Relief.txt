**Unpaid invoice? You can reclaim the VAT you paid.**

When a customer never pays, many UAE businesses absorb the VAT as well as the loss.

Article 64 of the VAT Decree-Law lets a registered supplier reduce its output tax for VAT already paid on a bad debt.

**All four conditions must be met:**
▶ **VAT charged and paid:** the supply was invoiced and the VAT declared in your return.
▶ **Written off:** the amount is written off as a bad debt in your accounts, in full or in part.
▶ **More than 6 months:** since the date of supply.
▶ **Customer notified:** you told the customer the amount written off, by letter or email.

The relief is limited to the VAT on the amount you actually wrote off. Write off half, recover half.

Your customer, if VAT-registered, must reverse the input tax it claimed on the unpaid amount.

✅ Run an ageing report and flag invoices older than six months.

✅ Send a written notice with the invoice number, date and amount written off, and keep proof.

Source: VAT Decree-Law Article 64 and FTA Public Clarification VATP024 (tax.gov.ae).
