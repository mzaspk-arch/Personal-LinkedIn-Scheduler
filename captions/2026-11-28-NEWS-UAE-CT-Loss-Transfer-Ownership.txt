**Two 50% owners can't share tax losses between their companies.**

The FTA issued Corporate Tax Public Clarifications CTP013 and CTP014 on 27 November 2026.

They explain the 75% ownership test for moving Tax Losses between UAE companies (Article 38(1)).

**What the FTA says:**
▶ **One owner only:** either company holds 75%+ of the other, or a single third person holds 75%+ of both. Holdings of several owners cannot be added together.
▶ **The FTA's own example:** Mr. C and Mr. D each own 50% of Company A and Company B. The test fails, so losses cannot move either way.
▶ **Continuity:** 75% must hold from the start of the period the loss arose to the end of the period it is used.
▶ **New companies:** a loss can only be used by a newly incorporated company if it existed at the start of the loss period.
▶ **Acquisitions:** buy a company on 1 July and neither side can transfer that year's loss.

Both clarifications apply from the start of Corporate Tax, so check past and current returns.

✅ Map who owns what, and since when, before you claim a group loss transfer.

✅ Plan acquisitions and new set-ups with this timing in mind.
