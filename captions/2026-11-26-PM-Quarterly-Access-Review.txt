**4 access checks your SaaS needs every quarter.**

Most SME data leaks do not need a hacker. They need an old login nobody switched off.

Every new app adds users, admins and shared passwords. Few businesses check them again.

**The quarterly review:**
▶ **Leavers:** removed from every app on their last day, including email, accounting, CRM and cloud storage.
▶ **No shared logins:** one named user for each person, so every action can be traced.
▶ **Two-factor login:** switched on for every user, not just the owner.
▶ **Admin rights:** kept to the few people who really need them.

It takes one hour with a simple list: app, user, role, last login.

The biggest risk is usually the finance and payroll systems, where one shared password can approve payments.

✅ Build an app-and-user list this week and give it an owner.

✅ Add "remove all app access" to your leaver checklist.

When did you last check who can still log in to your systems?
