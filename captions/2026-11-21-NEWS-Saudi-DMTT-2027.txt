**Saudi Arabia's 15% top-up tax starts 1 January 2027.**

ZATCA announced on 19 November 2026 that it will apply a Domestic Minimum Top-up Tax (DMTT) from that date.

It follows the OECD/G20 Pillar Two global minimum tax rules.

**Key points:**
▶ **Who:** constituent entities of multinational groups with consolidated annual revenue of EUR 750 million or more.
▶ **Revenue test:** at least two of the four fiscal years before the year the tax applies, based on the ultimate parent's consolidated statements.
▶ **How it works:** a top-up is collected in Saudi Arabia when profits are taxed below a 15% effective rate.
▶ **Not a revenue tax:** 15% is a minimum effective rate on profits.
▶ **Out of scope:** purely domestic companies with no branches outside Saudi Arabia that are not part of a covered group.

For groups with Saudi entities, top-up on low-taxed Saudi profits will now be collected in the Kingdom.
