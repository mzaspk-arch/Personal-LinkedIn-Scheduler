**The FTA now sets a step-by-step method for valuing deemed supplies.**

When a business uses services for non-business purposes or gives them away, VAT may be due as a deemed supply under Article 37 of the VAT Law. An FTA directive now sets out how to value it.

**An illustration (20% margin, 80% input-taxed cost ratio):**
▶ **Step 1:** start with the open market value of the service, say AED 100,000.
▶ **Step 2:** remove the profit margin. AED 100,000 ÷ 1.20 = AED 83,333.
▶ **Step 3:** apply the ratio of input-taxed costs to total costs. 80% of AED 83,333 = AED 66,667.
▶ **Step 4:** apply 5% VAT. The VAT due is AED 3,333.

**Watch for:**
▶ The method covers services only, not deemed supplies of goods.
▶ The FTA can compare your margin with your sector.
▶ A loss year or negative margin needs a documented position.

Prescribed methods leave less room for judgement, and more room for audit testing.
