**Every price you quote has four numbers.**

Lesson 15 from Gharebon Ka MBA with Dr. Zeeshan-ul-Hassan Usmani.

Your rate isn't a feeling, and it isn't what your cousin earns. The market publishes it daily.

**The price ladder:**
▶ **Floor:** what it truly costs you in hours and skill. Never quote below it.
▶ **Anchor:** what others charge. A reference point only; never let the cheapest decide your number.
▶ **Ceiling:** what solving the problem is worth to the buyer. Aim toward it.
▶ **Walk-away:** whether you can say no. This one quietly sets the other three.

Beginners hug the floor. Professionals climb toward the ceiling. The distance between them is confidence built on evidence.

The homework: collect twenty real prices from job adverts, freelance boards, three people already earning from your skill, and one buyer.

Twenty data points end an argument you've had with yourself for years.

✅ Write your floor, anchor and ceiling for your main service.

✅ Collect twenty real market prices before your next quote.

Which of the four numbers do you usually skip?
