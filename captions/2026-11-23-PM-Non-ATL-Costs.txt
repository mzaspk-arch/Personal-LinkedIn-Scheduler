**Off the ATL? Your withholding tax doubles or worse.**

Many people think staying off the Active Taxpayers' List (ATL) only costs a small penalty.

FBR's Withholding Tax Rate Card for tax year 2027 (Finance Act 2026) shows a very different picture.

**Three examples:**
▶ **Independent professionals** (doctors, lawyers, accountants, developers working independently): 15% withheld on the ATL, 30% off it.
▶ **New car, 1301–1600cc:** advance tax of 2% of the value on the ATL, 6% off it.
▶ **Cash withdrawals:** 0.8% advance tax, charged only to people off the ATL.

The same pattern runs across the card: most withholding rates are doubled for people not on the ATL, and some are tripled.

That is money deducted before it reaches you, often on every single payment.

✅ Check your status on the FBR ATL before your next large payment or purchase.

✅ If you missed the return deadline, file now so you can get back on the list.

Source: FBR Withholding Tax Rate Card, Tax Year 2027 (fbr.gov.pk).
