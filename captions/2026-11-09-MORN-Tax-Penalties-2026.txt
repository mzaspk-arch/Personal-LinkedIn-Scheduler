**Late tax now costs 14% a year, not 4% a month.**

Cabinet Decision No. 129 of 2025 rewrote the UAE penalty framework for VAT, Excise Tax and Corporate Tax from 14 April 2026.

The message is simple: correct errors early and the cost falls sharply.

**Key changes:**
▶ **Late payment:** 14% a year, accrued monthly on unpaid tax. This replaces 2% on the first day plus 4% a month.
▶ **Voluntary disclosure:** 1% a month on the tax difference until you file it.
▶ **After an audit notice:** a fixed 15% on top. The fixed element used to be 50%.
▶ **Incorrect return:** AED 500, waived if you correct it before the due date, or by a voluntary disclosure with no tax difference.
▶ **Records not in Arabic when requested:** AED 5,000, down from AED 20,000.

The cheapest time to fix a mistake is before the FTA finds it.

✅ Review your last four VAT returns for errors now.

✅ If you find one, file the voluntary disclosure before any audit notice arrives.
