**5 costs that UAE Corporate Tax will not deduct.**

Some expenses sit in your accounts as normal costs but must be added back in your Corporate Tax return.

Article 33 of the Corporate Tax Law lists them.

**The five that catch SMEs most:**
▶ **Fines and penalties:** including FTA and government fines. Only amounts awarded as compensation for damages or breach of contract are deductible.
▶ **Donations, grants or gifts:** to any entity that is not a Qualifying Public Benefit Entity.
▶ **Dividends and profit distributions:** paid to owners.
▶ **Corporate Tax and recoverable input VAT:** neither is a deductible cost.
▶ **Bribes or other illicit payments.**

Article 33 also blocks foreign tax on income and amounts withdrawn by a natural-person business owner.

✅ Map each of these to its own ledger account so the add-back is easy.

✅ Review your penalty and donation accounts before you close the year.

Source: Federal Decree-Law No. 47 of 2022, Article 33 (tax.gov.ae).
