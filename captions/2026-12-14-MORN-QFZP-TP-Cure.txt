**One pricing error need not cost your 0% Free Zone rate.**

A Qualifying Free Zone Person must comply with transfer pricing rules. A common worry: if one related-party transaction was booked above or below arm's length, is QFZP status lost?

The FTA's summary of private clarifications gives a practical answer.

**The approach:**
▶ If the transaction is recorded at arm's length in the accounts, no adjustment is needed.
▶ If it is not, an appropriate transfer pricing adjustment in the Corporate Tax return for that period can cure it, and the QFZP status need not be lost.
▶ Without that adjustment, the QFZP condition may fail.

**The evidence to keep:**
▶ A transfer pricing policy and benchmarking.
▶ Related-party agreements.
▶ A reconciliation from the accounts to the return.
▶ Management approval of the adjustment.

Private clarifications apply to the person who requested them, but they show how the FTA thinks.

✅ Review related-party pricing before you close the books, not after.

✅ Keep the adjustment file with the return.
