**March year-end? Corporate Tax due 31 December 2026.**

UAE Corporate Tax returns, and the tax due, must reach the FTA within 9 months of the end of the tax period.

If your financial year ended on 31 March 2026, that date is 31 December 2026.

Eight weeks sounds like plenty. Year-end holidays say otherwise.

**Before you file:**
▶ **File and pay:** both are due by 31 December 2026, through EmaraTax.
▶ **Audit:** financial statements must be audited if revenue is over AED 50 million or you are a Qualifying Free Zone Person.
▶ **Past errors:** errors in an earlier return worth up to AED 10,000 of tax can be corrected in the current return; larger ones need a Voluntary Disclosure.
▶ **Payment timing:** the FTA warns that bank transfers may not arrive instantly, and a late arrival counts as late payment.

✅ Book your auditor and finalise the accounts in November, not December.

✅ Pay a few working days before the deadline and keep the transfer confirmation.

Source: FTA Corporate Tax Returns guide: https://tax.gov.ae/Datafolder/Files/Guides/CT/CT-Returns-EN-11-11-2024.pdf
