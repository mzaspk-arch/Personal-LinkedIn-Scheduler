**Approvals on WhatsApp leave no audit trail.**

In many SMEs, payments and purchases are approved with a thumbs-up in a chat.

It feels fast. It is also one of the weakest controls in the business.

**Three reasons:**
▶ **Who approved what:** the approval sits in a chat, not on the invoice or purchase order. Months later, nobody can find it.
▶ **People leave:** phones change, chats are deleted and the history goes with them.
▶ **Limits:** nothing stops a payment above what the approver is allowed to sign off.

Your auditor, your tax authority and your bank all want the same thing: a clear record of who approved what, and when.

Most ERPs and workflow tools already do this, with approval limits per role and a log on every document.

✅ Pick one process this month, such as purchase orders or supplier payments, and move its approvals into your system.

✅ Set a simple limit per role, and send anything above it to the next level.
