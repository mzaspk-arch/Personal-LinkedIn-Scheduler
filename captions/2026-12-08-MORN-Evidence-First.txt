**UAE tax has moved from "claim now" to "evidence first".**

This is the last post in my series on the themes from PwC's Annual Tax Conference 2026. Looking back across all 30 topics, one pattern stands out.

**Three shifts:**
▶ **Substantiation comes first:** input VAT must be supported before the deduction, not rebuilt after an FTA query.
▶ **Prescription replaces judgement:** step-by-step valuation methods, fixed thresholds and set formulas leave less room to argue.
▶ **Documentation carries the audit:** written policies, named owners and records kept for the statutory period are now the baseline.

At the same time, long-running grey areas, such as composite supplies and cash payments, are being closed by clear rules.

For business owners this is good news. Clear rules are easier to plan for. But they reward businesses whose systems create the evidence automatically.
