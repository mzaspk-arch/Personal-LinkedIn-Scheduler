**One ERP used well beats five apps half-used.**

A typical SME stack grows one app at a time: invoicing here, inventory there, a CRM, a payroll tool and spreadsheets in between.

Each app solves one problem. Together they create a new one.

**Why I'd rather see one system used well:**
▶ **One version of the truth:** five apps mean five customer lists, five product codes and month-end spent reconciling them.
▶ **Hidden costs:** every new app adds a login, a licence, an export and someone who "owns" the integration.
▶ **Real value:** an ERP like Odoo pays off when sales, stock and accounts share one record, not when you buy more modules.

This doesn't mean "never add a tool".

It means: before adding one, ask whether the system you already pay for can do it.

✅ List every app your team logs into each week and what each one costs.

✅ Mark the ones that hold the same data twice. Those are your first candidates to merge.

Agree or disagree? How many apps does your finance team touch before month-end is closed?
