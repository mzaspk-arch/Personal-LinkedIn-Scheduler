**UAE VAT refunds are no longer automatic in 2026.**

The VAT and Tax Procedures amendments (Federal Decree-Laws No. 16 and 17 of 2025) took effect on 1 January 2026.

A refund claim is now reviewed, not simply paid.

**A VAT refund may be denied if:**
▶ **Audit:** your business is under FTA audit.
▶ **Risk:** risks or irregularities are identified.
▶ **Documents:** required information is incomplete.

Since 1 October 2026, input tax can also be refused if a purchase is linked to tax evasion anywhere in your supply chain.

**Excise rules changed too (Cabinet Decision No. 197 of 2025):**
▶ **Sugar-based tiers:** sweetened drinks are taxed by sugar per 100 ml, not a flat 50%.
▶ **Exclusions clarified:** for example, drinks with at least 75% milk, baby formula and medical drinks.
▶ **Stricter compliance:** an accredited lab report for each product decides its tier.

✅ Check your supplier due diligence and documents before your next refund claim.

✅ If you sell sweetened drinks, confirm every product has a valid lab report.
