**Life insurance is VAT-exempt. Are the fees around it?**

Directive on Tax Transactions No. 4 of 2026 answers when fees connected to a life insurance or reinsurance contract share its exemption.

**The conditions:**

▶ The service must be necessary for, and directly connected to, providing or transferring the contract.
▶ The fee must be included in the premium, with no separate charge.
▶ This can cover fees for managing, operating or executing the contract.
▶ Independent or separately charged services are separate supplies for VAT.
▶ The final answer depends on the facts of each case.

✅ Insurers: review how your fees are structured and billed.

✅ A separately invoiced fee can change the VAT outcome.
