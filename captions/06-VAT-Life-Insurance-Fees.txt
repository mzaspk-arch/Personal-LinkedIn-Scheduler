Life insurance is VAT-exempt. Are the fees around it?

Directive on Tax Transactions No. 4 of 2026 answers when fees connected to a life insurance or reinsurance contract share its exemption.

The conditions:

1. The service must be necessary for, and directly connected to, providing or transferring the contract.

2. The fee must be included in the premium, with no separate charge.

3. This can cover fees for managing, operating or executing the contract.

4. Independent or separately charged services are separate supplies for VAT.

5. The final answer depends on the facts of each case.

✅ Insurers: review how your fees are structured and billed.

✅ A separately invoiced fee can change the VAT outcome.
