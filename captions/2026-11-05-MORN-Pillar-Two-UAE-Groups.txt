**A UAE-focused group can still owe Top-up Tax.**

The UAE's 15% Domestic Minimum Top-up Tax applies to multinational groups with consolidated revenue of EUR 750 million or more in at least two of the four preceding years.

The word that catches local groups out is "multinational".

**What counts:**
▶ **A foreign subsidiary** that is consolidated makes the group multinational.
▶ **A foreign branch** that is a permanent establishment does too.
▶ **A foreign representative office** on its own usually does not.
▶ **Foreign JVs or minority stakes** that are not consolidated line by line do not.

So a large family group with one small branch abroad can be in scope.

Timing matters: where the first in-scope fiscal year ended before 30 April 2026, Top-up Tax registration is due by 30 November 2026.
