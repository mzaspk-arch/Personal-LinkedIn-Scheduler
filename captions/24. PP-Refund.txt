**Refund delayed? The cause usually starts before you file.**

Most refund delays begin long before the FTA reviews your claim.

They start with a missing invoice, a late return or an unanswered question.

The good news: each of these is in your control.

**6 steps that protect your refund eligibility:**
▶ **Verify first:** check every refund application carefully before filing.
▶ **File on time:** submit all tax returns accurately and within deadlines.
▶ **Keep records ready:** organise all supporting documents so you can produce them quickly.
▶ **Respond fast:** answer FTA inquiries without delay.
▶ **Cooperate in audits:** give full, clear cooperation throughout the process.
▶ **Tighten controls:** strengthen internal controls to reduce high-risk transactions.

Think of it this way: the FTA is checking whether your claim is supported.

The cleaner your records, the shorter that check tends to be.

✅ Before your next refund claim, match every figure to an invoice or contract.

✅ Assign one person to track and answer FTA requests as soon as they arrive.
