**Tourist VAT refunds now take one business day.**

On 20 October 2026 the FTA launched an AI-powered refund platform with Planet, the operator of the Tax Refund for Tourists Scheme.

It is live at Abu Dhabi, Dubai and Sharjah airports.

Tourists register in the Planet app, scan a QR code at the airport, and claims that need no inspection are finished in the app.

**What retailers should know:**
▶ **Refund time:** 1 business day, down from about 9.
▶ **Network:** about 16,700 retail outlets are connected to the scheme.
▶ **Growth:** 769 outlets joined in the first 9 months of 2026.
▶ **Forecast:** about 4 million refund transactions worth AED 692 million from November 2026 to October 2027.

Every refund starts with the tax-refund tag issued at your till.

A faster refund makes "tax-free" a stronger reason to buy from you.

✅ If you sell to tourists, check that every till is connected to the scheme.

✅ Train staff to offer and issue the refund tag at checkout, every time.
