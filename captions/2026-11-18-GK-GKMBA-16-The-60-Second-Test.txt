**Nobody can pay someone they can't find.**

Lesson 16 from Gharebon Ka MBA with Dr. Zeeshan-ul-Hassan Usmani.

The rule from class: if a stranger can't find evidence of your work in 60 seconds, you don't have a pricing problem. You have a visibility problem.

**The weakness that costs money is usually one of four:**
▶ **Not found:** the work is good but invisible. No profile, no portfolio.
▶ **Not verified:** no evidence a stranger can check in ten minutes.
▶ **Not asking:** you do the work and hope someone notices. They won't.
▶ **Not finishing:** plenty started, little shipped.

Promotions are decided in rooms you're not in. Contracts are awarded on calls you weren't invited to.

In both, the only version of you present is the one somebody else can describe.

Visibility isn't vanity. It's distribution, and distribution turns a skill into income.

✅ Search your own name as a stranger would. Time how long it takes to find real work.

✅ Circle the one weakness above that's most true for you.

Which of the four is holding you back most?
