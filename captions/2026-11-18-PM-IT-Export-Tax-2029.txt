**IT exporters: the 0.25% rate now runs to 2029.**

If you export IT or IT-enabled services from Pakistan, Finance Act 2026 brought good news and some changes around it.

**What changed:**
▶ **IT and IT-enabled services exports:** the reduced advance tax rate of 0.25% is extended to tax year 2029 (it was set to end in 2027).
▶ **Other exports:** advance tax on export proceeds is fixed at 1.25% as minimum tax.
▶ **Extra 1% under section 147(6C):** the additional adjustable advance tax on exporters is abolished.
▶ **Working independently for local clients:** withholding on independent professional services, including software engineers and developers, is 15%.

So the rate depends on who pays you and how. Export income and local client income are treated differently.

✅ Keep your export invoices and bank records of foreign proceeds together, invoice by invoice.

✅ If you also bill Pakistani clients, check the withholding on their payments to you.

Source: FBR Circular No. 02 of 2026-27, 8 September 2026: https://download1.fbr.gov.pk/Docs/202698139527880CircularNo.2of2026-27.pdf
