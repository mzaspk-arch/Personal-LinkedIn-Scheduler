**Selling property? Advance tax is now a flat rate.**

The Finance Act 2026 replaced the old slab-based advance tax on property deals in Pakistan.

FBR explained the changes in Circular No. 02 of 2026-27.

**What changed:**
▶ **Seller, section 236C:** a flat 2.75% of the gross amount of consideration received.
▶ **Buyer, section 236K:** a flat 1.25% of the fair market value of the property.
▶ **Late filers on the ATL:** the higher Tenth Schedule rates that applied to people who filed after the due date have been abolished.
▶ **Inherited property:** your cost is its fair market value when it is transferred to you. This matters when you later sell it and work out the gain.

The circular also cut advance tax on international credit, debit and prepaid cards from 5% to 0.5%.

✅ Before any deal, confirm both buyer and seller appear on the Active Taxpayers' List.

✅ For inherited property, keep the valuation from the date of transfer with your records.

Source: FBR Circular No. 02 of 2026-27: https://download1.fbr.gov.pk/Docs/202698139527880CircularNo.2of2026-27.pdf
