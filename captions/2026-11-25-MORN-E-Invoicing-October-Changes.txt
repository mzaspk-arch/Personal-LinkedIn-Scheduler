**E-invoice data no longer has to be stored in the UAE.**

Ministerial Decision No. 197 of 2026, effective 1 October 2026, made several practical changes to the e-invoicing rules before go-live.

**What changed:**
▶ **Data storage:** the requirement to keep e-invoice data inside the UAE has been removed. You must still keep records for the statutory period and make them available to the FTA on request.
▶ **Timing:** e-invoices and e-credit notes must be issued and transmitted within 14 business days of the transaction.
▶ **VAT groups:** transactions between registered members of the same VAT group are excluded from mandatory e-invoicing until 31 December 2028. Other group companies are not covered.
▶ **System failures:** the duty to notify the FTA within two business days has been removed.

This opens the door to cloud ERPs and ASPs hosted outside the UAE, but check the contract terms on access and retention.

✅ Ask your ERP and ASP where your invoice data is stored, and for how long.

✅ Confirm which group companies are actually inside your VAT group.
