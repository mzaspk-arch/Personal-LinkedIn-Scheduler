**Missed Corporate Tax registration? The AED 10,000 penalty can still go.**

The FTA waives the late registration penalty if you meet one timeline.

File your first Corporate Tax return within 7 months from the end of your first tax period.

Exempt persons: file your annual declaration within the same 7 months.

**Key points:**
▶ **Who it covers:** UAE corporate taxpayers, and exempt persons required to file annual declarations.
▶ **Registered late?** You still qualify if you meet the filing timeline.
▶ **Already paid the penalty?** It is refunded or credited to your tax account.
▶ **Where:** registration and filing are done on EmaraTax.

Example: a first tax period ending 31 March 2026 means filing by 31 October 2026.

Miss the 7 months and the AED 10,000 penalty stays.
