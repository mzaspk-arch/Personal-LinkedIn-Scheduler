**"Double your income every year" breaks on square 30.**

Lesson 04 from Gharebon Ka MBA with Dr. Zeeshan-ul-Hassan Usmani.

**The myth:** double every year and you'll be rich in a decade.

The old chessboard story shows why.

A courtier asks a king for one grain of rice on the first square, doubled on every square after.

**What the king actually agreed to:**
▶ **Square 30:** about 13 tonnes of rice, owed for one square.
▶ **Square 40:** about 14,000 tonnes.
▶ **Square 64:** roughly four centuries of the world's rice harvest.

Doubling curves look fine on paper. Real markets are finite.

Compounding is real. But it is slow, back-loaded, and needs a surplus most people don't have yet.

That's why the course starts with earning, not investing. Skill is the one asset that compounds from zero.

✅ Be wary of any plan that only shows the lower half of the board.

✅ Invest first in a skill someone already pays for.

Have you ever been sold a doubling promise?
