**A UAE visa doesn't make you tax resident.**

Many people assume a residence visa is all it takes.

It isn't. For individuals, UAE tax residency is decided by Cabinet Decision No. 85 of 2022.

**You meet one of these tests:**
▶ **183 days:** you were physically in the UAE for 183 days or more in the relevant 12 months.
▶ **90 days plus:** you were here for 90 days or more, hold a residence permit (or are a UAE or GCC national), and have a permanent home or a job or business here.
▶ **Main home:** your usual or primary residence and the centre of your financial and personal interests are in the UAE.

You prove it with a Tax Residency Certificate from the FTA.

**Know before you apply:**
▶ It covers a current or past 12-month period, never a future one.
▶ Fees start at AED 50 to submit, plus AED 500 to AED 1,750 for review, depending on who applies.
▶ The FTA aims to issue it within 10 business days of a complete application.

✅ Download your entry and exit report before you apply.

✅ If you also earn abroad, check what the relevant tax treaty requires.

Source: FTA Tax Residency Certificate service: https://www.tax.gov.ae/en/services/issuance.of.tax.certificates.aspx
