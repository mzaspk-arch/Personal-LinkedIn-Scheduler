**Audit stress usually starts with your records, not the auditor.**

Most audit delays come from the same place: missing support, unreconciled balances and IFRS questions left to the last week.

In the UAE, audited financial statements are no longer optional for many businesses.

**Who needs them under Corporate Tax:**
▶ **Revenue above AED 50 million:** audited financial statements are required.
▶ **Qualifying Free Zone Persons:** required to keep the 0% rate.

Many free zone and mainland authorities also ask for them at licence renewal.

**What makes an audit simple:**
▶ **Monthly reconciliations:** bank, receivables, payables and VAT.
▶ **IFRS choices documented:** revenue, leases and provisions agreed before year-end.
▶ **One evidence folder:** contracts, invoices and approvals in one place.

✅ Close each month properly, not just the year.

✅ Book a pre-audit review two months before your year-end.

This is how our team approaches statutory and internal audits for free zone and mainland companies.
