**Myth: a free zone licence means zero tax, no questions asked.**

Fact: the 0% Corporate Tax rate is a benefit you earn and keep, not one you get automatically.

**To stay a Qualifying Free Zone Person:**
▶ **Activity:** 0% applies only to qualifying income; other income is taxed at 9%.
▶ **Threshold:** non-qualifying revenue must stay within the de minimis limit (the lower of AED 5 million or 5% of revenue).
▶ **Substance:** you need real staff, premises and activity in the free zone.
▶ **Records:** you must keep audited financial statements and follow transfer pricing rules.

Miss one condition and you can lose the 0% rate for that year and the next four.

**Two more points people forget:**
▶ **Filing:** you still register for Corporate Tax and file a return every year.
▶ **VAT:** most free zones are treated like the mainland for VAT, so registration and 5% VAT can still apply.

✅ Review your income streams against the qualifying activities list.

✅ Check your non-qualifying revenue before your year-end, not after.
