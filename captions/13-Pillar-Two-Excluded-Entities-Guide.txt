**Exempt from Corporate Tax doesn't mean exempt from Top-up Tax.**

The FTA's guide TTGEIE1 explains which entities sit outside the Domestic Minimum Top-up Tax.

**Key points:**

▶ Excluded Entities include international organisations, non-profits, pension funds, and investment funds or REIVs that are the Ultimate Parent Entity.
▶ Subsidiaries can qualify at 95%+ ownership with holding activities, or 85%+ with substantially all income excluded.
▶ Ownership is measured by value, not share count.
▶ Corporate Tax exemption and Excluded Entity status are separate tests.
▶ Excluded entities don't register or file, but their revenue still counts towards the EUR 750m threshold.
