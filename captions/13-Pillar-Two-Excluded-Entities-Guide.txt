Exempt from Corporate Tax doesn't mean exempt from Top-up Tax.

The FTA's guide TTGEIE1 explains which entities sit outside the Domestic Minimum Top-up Tax.

Key points:

1. Excluded Entities include international organisations, non-profits, pension funds, and investment funds or REIVs that are the Ultimate Parent Entity.

2. Subsidiaries can qualify at 95%+ ownership with holding activities, or 85%+ with substantially all income excluded.

3. Ownership is measured by value, not share count.

4. Corporate Tax exemption and Excluded Entity status are separate tests.

5. Excluded entities don't register or file, but their revenue still counts towards the EUR 750m threshold.
