**A monthly close is now a tax control.**

Many UAE SMEs still close their books once a year, when the auditor or the Corporate Tax return forces them to.

I think that habit is getting expensive.

**Three reasons:**
▶ **E-invoicing:** your invoice data will reach the FTA through your Accredited Service Provider. What you report and what sits in your books must match.
▶ **VAT:** when ledgers are reconciled every month, each VAT return becomes a check, not a rebuild from scratch.
▶ **Corporate Tax:** errors found in month two cost far less to fix than errors found at year-end, or by the FTA.

A monthly close doesn't need a big team. It needs a short checklist and the discipline to finish it.

✅ Reconcile bank, VAT control and receivables accounts by the 10th of every month.

✅ Review one simple profit and loss against last month before anything is filed.
