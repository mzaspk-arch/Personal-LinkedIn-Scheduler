**Top-up Tax: register by 30 November 2026.**

FTA Decision No. 12 of 2026 sets the timelines for UAE entities of multinational groups.

**The deadlines:**

▶ Register within 7 months of the end of the first in-scope Fiscal Year.
▶ Fiscal Year ended before 30 April 2026? Register by 30 November 2026.
▶ Deregister within 6 months of ceasing to exist or leaving the group.
▶ Entity ceased before 30 June 2026? Deregister by 31 December 2026.
▶ Group fell out of scope? Notify within 6 months. Back in scope? Notify within 7 months.

You can't deregister until all Top-up Tax and penalties are paid and all returns are filed.
