**Qatar moved its Pillar Two registration deadline to 31 January 2027.**

Qatar's General Tax Authority announced the extension on 4 November 2026.

It covers registration for the Global and Domestic Minimum Tax through the Dhareeba platform.

**What the announcement says:**
▶ **Who:** multinational enterprise groups and joint venture groups subject to the Global and Domestic Minimum Tax.
▶ **Where:** registration is completed on Dhareeba.
▶ **Appoint:** each group names a Designated Local Entity.
▶ **Its role:** handling registration, notification and tax return filing obligations in Qatar.
▶ **New deadline:** 31 January 2027.

The registration service has been open since 2 August 2026.

The GTA's stated aim is to give groups more time to prepare the required data and information.

An extension is breathing room, not a reason to wait.

✅ Confirm which group entities in Qatar are in scope and who will act as the Designated Local Entity.

✅ Collect the group data Dhareeba asks for now, so registration is done well before 31 January.
