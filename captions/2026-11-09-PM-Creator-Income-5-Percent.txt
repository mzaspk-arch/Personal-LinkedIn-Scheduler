**YouTube income? Your bank now deducts 5%.**

Many creators still believe platform income is outside Pakistan's tax net.

The Finance Act 2026 changed that with a new section 154B of the Income Tax Ordinance.

**How it works:**
▶ **Who deducts:** every bank and non-banking financial institution, when revenue from a social media platform is credited to your account.
▶ **The rate:** 5% of the amount received.
▶ **Residents:** the 5% is a minimum tax. You still declare the income in your return.
▶ **Non-residents without a permanent establishment in Pakistan:** the 5% is a final tax.

The law defines "digital content creator" and "social media influencer" for this purpose, so YouTubers, TikTokers and other influencers are in scope.

✅ Check your bank statements for the 5% deduction and keep the tax certificates.

✅ Claim the deducted tax in your return so it is not paid twice.

Source: FBR Circular No. 02 of 2026-27 (Finance Act 2026 amendments): https://download1.fbr.gov.pk/Docs/202698139527880CircularNo.2of2026-27.pdf
