**Fair-value investment property can still earn a tax deduction.**

Under the fair value model, there is no accounting depreciation. Ministerial Decision No. 173 of 2025 lets eligible businesses claim a tax deduction instead, for tax periods starting on or after 1 January 2025.

**The conditions:**
▶ **Accounts:** prepared on the accrual basis, with investment property measured at fair value under IAS 40.
▶ **Election:** the realisation basis election under Article 20(3) must also be made. Both elections can be made in the same return.
▶ **Assets:** buildings or parts of buildings held for rent or capital growth. Land does not qualify.
▶ **Amount:** each year, the lower of 4% of original cost or the opening tax written-down value, prorated for shorter or longer periods.
▶ **Timing:** the election covers the whole portfolio, is irrevocable, and must be made in the first relevant tax period. Miss it and the relief is lost.

For property-holding companies this can be a meaningful, recurring deduction.
