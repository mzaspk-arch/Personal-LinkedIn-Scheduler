**New excise floors for tobacco and vapes from 1 September.**

Cabinet Decision No. 137 of 2026 sets minimum Excise Prices, the value excise tax is calculated on.

**The floors:**

▶ Cigarettes, normal or electrically heated: at least AED 0.40 per cigarette.
▶ **Water pipe tobacco and similar products:** at least AED 0.10 per gram.
▶ **E-cigarette liquids:** at least AED 1 per ml, with or without nicotine.
▶ These are minimum tax bases, not tax rates or retail prices.
▶ The Decision replaces Cabinet Decision No. 55 of 2019.

The Excise Price is still calculated as before, but it can't fall below these floors.

✅ Producers and importers: check every product against the new floors.

✅ Include nicotine-free e-liquids in your review.
