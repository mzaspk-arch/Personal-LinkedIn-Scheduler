**UAE Top-up Tax: first filings are due 30 June 2027.**

For groups with a calendar financial year, FY 2025 is the first year of UAE Top-up Tax.

**The timing:**
▶ **First year:** the return and payment are due 18 months after the end of the first fiscal year. For FY ending 31 December 2025, that is 30 June 2027.
▶ **Later years:** 15 months after the fiscal year end.
▶ **Payment:** UAE Top-up Tax is paid in AED by the return due date.

**Who files:**
▶ Ministerial Decision No. 133 of 2026 sets out which UAE entities must file the Pillar Two Information Return, for fiscal years starting on or after 1 January 2025.
▶ A Designated Local Entity can file on behalf of the other UAE group entities.
▶ Where a qualifying information return is filed abroad by the parent, the UAE filing can be simpler, but the FTA must still be notified.

Eighteen months sounds long. It is not, once you add data collection, GloBE adjustments and audit sign-off.
