**Three tests can spare you the full Pillar Two calculation.**

The Transitional CbCR Safe Harbour lets large groups use Country-by-Country Report data to show that no Top-up Tax is due in a jurisdiction. For calendar-year groups it generally covers FY 2025 to FY 2027.

**Pass any one test:**
▶ **De minimis:** revenue below EUR 10 million and profit before tax below EUR 1 million in that jurisdiction.
▶ **Simplified ETR:** simplified covered taxes divided by profit before tax is at least 16% for 2025 and 17% for 2026.
▶ **Routine profit:** profit before tax is at or below the substance-based income exclusion.

**Two warnings:**
▶ The data must come from a Qualified CbC Report and qualified financial statements.
▶ "Once out, always out": if you do not use the safe harbour for a jurisdiction in a year, you cannot return to it there later.

A UAE operation paying 9% Corporate Tax will usually fail the ETR test, so the other two tests matter more here.

✅ Run all three tests on your 2025 figures for each jurisdiction.

✅ Document why each test is met or failed.
