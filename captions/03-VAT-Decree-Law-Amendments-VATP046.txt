**5 VAT law changes every UAE business should know.**

The FTA's Public Clarification VATP046 explains recent amendments to the VAT Decree-Law.

**Here is what matters for your books:**

▶ Excess recoverable VAT must now be used or refunded within 5 years from the end of the Tax Period it arose in. This applies from 1 January 2026.
▶ **Reverse-charge imports:** you no longer issue a tax invoice to yourself, but you still account for the VAT.
▶ Input tax can be rejected if your supply chain is linked to tax evasion and you knew, or should have known.
▶ E-invoicing is now part of the VAT law. Obligations apply as the phased rollout reaches your business.
▶ E-invoices issued through the system must be kept electronically.

✅ Review old VAT credit balances against the 5-year limit.

✅ Document your supplier checks. "I didn't know" is no longer enough.
