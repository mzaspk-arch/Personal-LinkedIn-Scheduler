5 VAT law changes every UAE business should know.

The FTA's Public Clarification VATP046 explains recent amendments to the VAT Decree-Law.

Here is what matters for your books:

1. Excess recoverable VAT must now be used or refunded within 5 years from the end of the Tax Period it arose in. This applies from 1 January 2026.

2. Reverse-charge imports: you no longer issue a tax invoice to yourself, but you still account for the VAT.

3. Input tax can be rejected if your supply chain is linked to tax evasion and you knew, or should have known.

4. E-invoicing is now part of the VAT law. Obligations apply as the phased rollout reaches your business.

5. E-invoices issued through the system must be kept electronically.

✅ Review old VAT credit balances against the 5-year limit.

✅ Document your supplier checks. "I didn't know" is no longer enough.
