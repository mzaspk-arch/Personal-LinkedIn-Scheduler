**Small company? Transfer pricing still applies to you.**

A common myth in UAE SMEs: transfer pricing is only for big multinationals.

Under the UAE Corporate Tax Law, the arm's length principle applies to every business that deals with related parties or connected persons.

**What actually depends on size:**
▶ **Arm's length (Article 34):** prices with related parties must match what independent parties would agree. No size limit.
▶ **Master file and local file:** only if your revenue is AED 200 million or more, or your multinational group's revenue is AED 3.15 billion or more (Ministerial Decision No. 97 of 2023).
▶ **Below those limits:** no formal files, but the FTA can still ask you to show your prices are at market value (Article 55).

Think of sales to a sister company, a loan from the owner's other business or shared staff costs.
