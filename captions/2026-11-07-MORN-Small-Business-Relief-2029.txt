**Small Business Relief has been extended to 31 December 2029.**

Ministerial Decision No. 131 of 2026 replaced the earlier end date of 31 December 2026.

The conditions have not changed.

**To use the relief:**
▶ **Residence:** you must be a Resident Person for Corporate Tax, either a company or an individual.
▶ **Revenue:** AED 3 million or less in the current tax period and in every previous tax period. Exceed it once and later periods are out.
▶ **Election:** you must elect it in each Corporate Tax return. It is not automatic.
▶ **Timing:** it covers tax periods ending on or before 31 December 2029.

Qualifying Free Zone Persons and members of large multinational groups cannot use it.

With the relief, you are treated as having no taxable income for that period. You still register, keep records and file a return.

✅ Check your revenue for every tax period since you came into Corporate Tax.

✅ Make sure whoever prepares your return makes the election each year.
