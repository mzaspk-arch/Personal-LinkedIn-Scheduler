**FBR just replaced your sales tax return format.**

FBR issued S.R.O. 2271(I)/2026 on 23 November 2026.

It substitutes Form STR-7, Annex-A (domestic purchase invoices) and Annex-C (domestic sales invoices) under rule 14 of the Sales Tax Rules, 2006.

In plain terms: FBR now wants more detail behind every invoice you report.

**New details to report:**
▶ **Purchases:** your supplier's return-filing status and the HS code.
▶ **Input tax:** a reason for any input tax that is not creditable.
▶ **Sales:** buyer registration, where the sale originates and its destination.
▶ **Special rates:** the SRO or Schedule reference for exempt, zero-rated or reduced-rate supplies.
▶ **Barcode:** a field on both annexes.

If your accounting system cannot export these fields, your team will be typing them by hand at month-end.

✅ Compare your invoice data and ERP export with the new Annex-A and Annex-C columns.

✅ Clean up supplier and customer master data (registration numbers, HS codes, provinces) before your next return.
