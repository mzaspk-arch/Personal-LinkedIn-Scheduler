**The AED 375K VAT test is not your year-end.**

A common belief: "We'll register for VAT once our annual revenue passes AED 375,000."

The test doesn't wait for your financial year.

**How the FTA test works:**
▶ **Look back:** register if your taxable supplies and imports exceeded AED 375,000 over the previous 12 months. Any rolling 12 months, not your financial year.
▶ **Look forward:** register if you expect to exceed AED 375,000 in the next 30 days. One large contract can trigger this.
▶ **Voluntary option:** you may register if taxable supplies and imports, or taxable expenses, exceed AED 187,500.
▶ **Non-residents:** a business without a UAE place of residence has no threshold when no one else must account for the VAT.

Registering late means VAT you didn't charge can still be due, plus penalties.

✅ Add a monthly check: total taxable sales and imports for the last 12 months.

✅ Before signing a big contract, check whether it pushes you over AED 375,000 within 30 days.

Source: https://tax.gov.ae/en/taxes/Vat/Vat.Topics/registration.for.vat.aspx
