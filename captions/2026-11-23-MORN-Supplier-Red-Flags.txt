**Five supplier red flags your team should escalate, not ignore.**

Under FTA Decision No. 13 of 2026, you must verify suppliers and supplies before deducting input tax. Part of that is spotting risk indicators.

**Escalate when you see:**
▶ **Frequent changes** of address or key people at the supplier.
▶ **Pricing out of line** with the market, far too low or far too high.
▶ **Cash or third-party payments**, where you are asked to pay someone other than the supplier.
▶ **Offshore payment routes** for a UAE supply.
▶ **Mismatched details**, where the TRN, trade licence or address do not match the invoice.

A red flag does not mean you cannot buy. It means someone senior reviews it and the outcome is recorded.

**Good governance looks like this:**
▶ A written verification policy.
▶ A named owner for each check.
▶ Evidence kept for the statutory period: generally five years, or fifteen for real estate.

✅ Add these five flags to your supplier onboarding form this week.

✅ Keep an escalation log showing how each flag was resolved.
