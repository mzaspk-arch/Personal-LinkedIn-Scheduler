**Customise your ERP last, not first.**

Many ERP projects go wrong in the first month.

Not because of the software, but because the team starts changing it before using it.

**Why I'd hold back on customisation:**
▶ **Standard first:** run the out-of-the-box flow for a month. Many "must-have" changes disappear once people learn the standard way.
▶ **Configure before you code:** in Odoo and similar systems, settings, fields and approval rules often close the gap without a developer.
▶ **Every custom module has a cost:** it must be tested again at each upgrade, and someone has to maintain it long after go-live.

Customisation isn't wrong. It should solve a real business problem, not reproduce an old habit.

✅ Keep a list of requested changes for the first 30 days, then review which still matter.

✅ For each change, ask: is this a setting, a report or real code?
