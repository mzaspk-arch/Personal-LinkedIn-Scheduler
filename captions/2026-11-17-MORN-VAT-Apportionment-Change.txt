**Your VAT recovery ratio is changing, and could move either way.**

Cabinet Decision No. 149 of 2026 changes the standard method of input tax apportionment.

From the first tax year starting on or after 1 October 2027, the ratio will be based on the value of your outputs, not on your input tax.

**Who feels it most:**
▶ **Banks and insurers** with large exempt income.
▶ **Holding companies and investment structures** with out-of-scope income.
▶ **Mixed businesses** where exempt and taxable activities share costs.

**An illustration:** on AED 100,000 of residual input VAT, moving from 60% to 80% recovery means AED 20,000 more recovered. A move the other way costs the same.

Government entities and charities have separate rules.

✅ Model your recovery under both methods using this year's numbers.

✅ Review any special method the FTA approved for you. It may need revisiting.

Source: Cabinet Decision No. 149 of 2026, Ministry of Finance (mof.gov.ae).
