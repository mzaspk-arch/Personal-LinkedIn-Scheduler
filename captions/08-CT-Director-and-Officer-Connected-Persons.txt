Director or officer? It's about authority, not job title.

Under Article 36 of the Corporate Tax Law, payments to directors and officers are deductible only at Market Value.

Public Clarification CTP010 explains who counts:

1. Director: a member of the board, or equivalent body, under the constitutional documents.

2. A "director" job title alone doesn't make someone a director.

3. Officer: someone with authority to plan, direct and control, make strategic decisions, or bind the company.

4. Conduct counts. Exercising that authority makes you an officer, whatever your title.

5. Only a natural person can be a director or officer.

✅ List everyone with real decision-making power, not only the board.

✅ Test their pay and benefits against Market Value before you file.
