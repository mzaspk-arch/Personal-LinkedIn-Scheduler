**Tax losses won't wipe out next year's tax.**

A common assumption: "We made losses, so next year's profit is covered."

Under UAE Corporate Tax, that is only partly true.

The FTA's bulletin on Corporate Tax losses (25 June 2026) sets out the rules.

**What the rules say:**
▶ **75% cap:** losses can offset at most 75% of taxable income in a tax period. Oldest losses go first.
▶ **No choice:** you must use losses up to that 75% limit. You can't hold them back for later.
▶ **What doesn't count:** losses before 1 June 2023, or before you became a taxable person, can't be carried forward.
▶ **Ownership change:** if more than 50% of ownership changes, you keep the losses only if you carry on the same or a similar business.
▶ **Small Business Relief:** in a year you elect it, carried-forward losses can't be used.

The FTA's example: AED 1,000,000 of taxable income and AED 3,000,000 of losses leaves AED 250,000 taxable.

✅ Keep a tax loss schedule by year, separate from your accounting losses.

✅ Check the loss position before any share sale or new investor comes in.

Source: FTA Basic Tax Information Bulletin, Corporate Tax Losses: https://tax.gov.ae/Datafolder/Files/Pdf/2026/CT-Tax-Bulletin-Corporate-Tax-Losses-EN.pdf
