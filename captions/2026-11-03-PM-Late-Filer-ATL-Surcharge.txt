**Missed 31 October? File now, then pay to rejoin ATL.**

Tax year 2026 income tax returns were due by 31 October 2026, after two extensions.

If you file after that date, your name stays off the Active Taxpayers' List until you pay the section 182A surcharge.

Being off the ATL means non-filer withholding rates on everyday transactions.

The Finance Act 2026 raised the surcharge sharply.

**The new amounts:**
▶ **Individual:** Rs 25,000 (was Rs 1,000)
▶ **Association of persons:** Rs 50,000 (was Rs 10,000)
▶ **Company:** Rs 100,000 (was Rs 20,000)

There is one new option for individuals.

You can give the Commissioner an undertaking that you will not buy or acquire any property for six months. The surcharge condition then does not apply.

✅ File the return on IRIS first. The surcharge is paid on top of it, not instead of it.

✅ Pay through a PSID under the "Misc" payment head, then check your status by SMS to 9966.

Source: FBR Circular No. 02 of 2026-27 (Finance Act 2026 amendments): https://download1.fbr.gov.pk/Docs/202698139527880CircularNo.2of2026-27.pdf
