**380 or 480? The MoF just clarified your e-invoice type.**

The UAE Ministry of Finance released version 2.1 of the Electronic Invoicing Guidelines, dated 25 November 2026.

Its updated Appendix 6 settles questions many ERP teams have been arguing about.

**What it says:**
▶ **Code 380 (Tax Invoice):** invoices containing taxable supplies where UAE VAT applies.
▶ **Code 480 (Commercial Invoice):** out-of-scope transactions, and invoices made up entirely of VAT-exempt and/or non-VAT supplies.
▶ **The test is the VAT treatment,** not only whether the supplier is VAT registered or where it is established.
▶ **Outside VAT is not outside e-invoicing:** only the Chapter 7 exclusions remove the obligation.
▶ **Retention credit notes:** reason code DL8.61.1.C (consideration altered) may be used.
▶ **Reverse charge lines:** report the GTIN where the item is identified by one (issued by GS1 UAE).

Businesses with revenue of AED 50 million or more must go live on 1 January 2027.

✅ Map every tax code in your ERP to 380 or 480 using these rules.

✅ Ask your ASP to test exempt-only and out-of-scope invoices before go-live.

Source: https://mof.gov.ae/en/about-us/initiatives/einvoicing/
