**UAE R&D can now earn a tax credit up to 50%.**

The R&D tax credit applies for tax periods starting on or after 1 January 2026, under Cabinet Decision No. 215 of 2025 and Ministerial Decision No. 24 of 2026.

**How the rates work:**
▶ **15%** on qualifying spend up to AED 1 million, with at least 2 R&D staff.
▶ **35%** on spend between AED 1 million and AED 2 million, with at least 6 R&D staff.
▶ **50%** on spend between AED 2 million and AED 5 million, with at least 14 R&D staff.

If a staff condition is missed, the rate drops to the tier where both conditions are met.

**Example:** AED 900,000 of qualifying spend with 3 R&D staff gives a credit of AED 135,000.

Other key points: at least AED 500,000 per project, a 30% uplift on qualifying staff costs, and the credit is not refundable in this first phase.

For software businesses like the ones I build, this changes the maths of hiring developers in the UAE.

✅ Separate R&D staff time and costs in your payroll and project records now.

✅ Track R&D headcount, because the staff test decides the rate.
