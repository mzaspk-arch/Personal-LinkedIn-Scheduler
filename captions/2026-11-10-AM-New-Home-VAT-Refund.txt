**Building your home? Claim the VAT back within 12 months.**

UAE nationals building a new home for their family can recover the VAT paid on construction.

On 30 October 2026 the FTA confirmed the improvements made to this refund service.

**What has changed:**
▶ **More time:** the deadline is now 12 months from completion, up from 6 months.
▶ **More costs covered:** for claims submitted from 1 January 2026, eligible items include staff quarters, home gyms, swimming pools, landscaping and integrated security or smart-home systems, if they are part of the residence on the same plot.
▶ **Simpler process:** the FTA says application fields are down 50% and the time to apply is down 80%, with the Maskan app.

**Documents to prepare:**
▶ Family Book and Emirates ID
▶ Construction contract and completion certificate
▶ Proof the home is occupied, such as a utility bill

✅ Keep every contractor and supplier tax invoice in one folder from day one.

✅ Note your completion date. The 12 months start from there.

Source: FTA, 30 October 2026: https://tax.gov.ae/en/media.centre/news/reviewing.the.development.of.the.vat.refund.service.for.uae.nationals.building.new.residences.as.a.model.for.the.zero.bureaucracy.programme.aspx
