**How a 9% tax rate becomes a 15% tax bill.**

Here is a simplified UAE Top-up Tax calculation for one entity in a large multinational group, financial year 2025.

**The facts:**
▶ Pillar Two income: AED 300 million.
▶ Covered taxes: AED 27 million.
▶ Eligible payroll: AED 40 million.
▶ Eligible tangible assets: AED 50 million.

**The steps:**
▶ **Effective tax rate:** 27 ÷ 300 = 9%.
▶ **Top-up percentage:** 15% minus 9% = 6%.
▶ **Substance carve-out (2025 rates):** 9.6% of payroll (AED 3.84m) plus 7.6% of assets (AED 3.8m) = AED 7.64m.
▶ **Excess profit:** AED 300m minus AED 7.64m = AED 292.36m.
▶ **Top-up Tax:** 6% of AED 292.36m = AED 17.54 million.

The real calculation starts from adjusted accounting profit and adjusted taxes, not from the tax return.

The carve-out rewards real people and real assets in the UAE, but its rates fall every year.
