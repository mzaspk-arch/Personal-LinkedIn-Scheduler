**Splitting invoices below AED 10,000 will not avoid supplier checks.**

FTA Decision No. 13 of 2026 lets you skip verification for individual supplies below AED 10,000, excluding VAT.

But the exception has a limit.

**A simple example:**
▶ A supplier sends 13 invoices of AED 8,000 each.
▶ Each one is below AED 10,000.
▶ Together they reach AED 104,000 from the same supplier.

Once supplies from one supplier exceed AED 100,000 in 12 months, or are expected to, the small-supply exception no longer applies to that supplier.

So the question is not "how big is this invoice?" but "how much will we buy from this supplier this year?"

Frequent small invoices from one supplier can also look like invoice splitting during an audit.

✅ Track spend per supplier on a rolling 12-month basis, not per invoice.

✅ Verify any supplier you expect to pass AED 100,000 before the next claim.
