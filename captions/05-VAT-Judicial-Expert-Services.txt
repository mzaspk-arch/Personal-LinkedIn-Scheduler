**Court-appointed experts: your fees are subject to VAT.**

Directive on Tax Transactions No. 1 of 2026 settles the question.

Services by a Judicial Expert appointed by a competent court:

▶ Are supplied in the course of Business in the UAE.
▶ Are Taxable Supplies under the VAT Law.
▶ Any amount received is Consideration for a supply of services.
▶ The expert must register for VAT where the registration requirements are met.
▶ It makes no difference if a Government Entity pays.

A Judicial Expert is a person on the roster of experts with the Ministry of Justice, a local judicial authority or an arbitration centre.

✅ If you act as a court-appointed expert, check whether your income crosses the registration threshold.

✅ Account for VAT on these engagements from now on.
