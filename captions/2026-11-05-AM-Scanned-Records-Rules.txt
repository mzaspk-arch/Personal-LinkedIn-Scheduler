**Your scanned records must match the original.**

Many businesses keep invoices as phone photos or partial scans.

FTA Decision No. 4 of 2026 sets the rules for keeping accounting records and commercial books as electronic copies or photocopies.

**What the FTA now expects:**
▶ **Complete:** the copy must be identical to the original, with every page in the same order. Partial scans are not accepted.
▶ **Legible:** data must be clear on a computer screen, and photocopies must not fade during the record-keeping period.
▶ **Accessible:** if files or systems are password-protected or encrypted, you must give the FTA the passwords or keys.
▶ **Your responsibility:** you can use a third party to store records, but you remain legally responsible for them.

How long? Generally 5 years after the tax period, and 7 years for real estate records, under the Tax Procedures Executive Regulation. Audits and disputes can extend this.

✅ Check one month of scanned invoices: all pages, readable, in order?

✅ If an outsourced provider stores your records, confirm in writing how the FTA would get access.
