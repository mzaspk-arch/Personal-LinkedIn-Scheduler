**Free Zone distribution: your customer's use of the goods decides.**

Distribution in or from a Designated Zone is a qualifying activity only where goods go to customers who resell them, or resell products that contain them.

The FTA's private clarifications summary draws a clear line.

**The test:**
▶ **Resale:** the customer sells the goods on, or products containing them. The distribution activity can qualify.
▶ **End use:** the customer keeps the goods, for example machines it uses to provide its own services. That is an end user, so it does not qualify.
▶ **Other suppliers:** a customer that also buys from other distributors can still be a reseller.
▶ **Pricing:** the pricing mechanism does not decide whether the activity qualifies.

**Due diligence to keep:**
▶ KYC and a customer profile.
▶ A contract or undertaking confirming resale.
▶ A review of how the products are used, refreshed periodically, with exceptions escalated.

From 2026 an auditor's report must also confirm customer status, so this evidence matters even more.
