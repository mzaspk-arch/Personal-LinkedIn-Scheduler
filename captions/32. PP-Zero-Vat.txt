**Messy books are the biggest cause of VAT stress.**

Most VAT problems for SMEs do not start in the return.

They start months earlier, in the bookkeeping.

**Where it usually goes wrong:**
▶ **Missing invoices:** input VAT you cannot support is input VAT you cannot recover.
▶ **Wrong coding:** standard-rated, zero-rated and exempt sales mixed in one account.
▶ **Late reconciliations:** bank and VAT accounts only checked at return time.
▶ **No review step:** figures go into the return without a second look.

Clean, VAT-compliant books fix this before the deadline arrives.
