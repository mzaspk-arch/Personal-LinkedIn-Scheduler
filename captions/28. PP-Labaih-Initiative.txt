**Does a parent or relative of yours deal with the FTA?**

The Federal Tax Authority runs the Labaih Initiative for senior citizens.

It gives them tailored, secure and simpler access to tax services, with full respect for their privacy.

**What it includes:**
▶ **Call Centre:** priority access when they phone the FTA.
▶ **Taxpayer Support Centres:** a fast-track service and faster processing.
▶ **Direct support line:** a dedicated team that answers their questions.
▶ **Digital access:** documents available on the FTA website, with no visit needed.
▶ **Awareness sessions:** workshops that explain their tax obligations in plain terms.

Why it matters for business owners too:

If a senior family member is the registered person for a business, their applications, requests and compliance steps can be handled with less waiting and more guidance.

✅ Check whose name each tax registration in your family or business sits under.

✅ If it is a senior family member, use the Labaih priority channels for their next request.
