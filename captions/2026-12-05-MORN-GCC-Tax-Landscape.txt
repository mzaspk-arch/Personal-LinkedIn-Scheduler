**The GCC is moving from introducing taxes to enforcing them.**

For regional groups, the UAE is only one part of the picture.

**Where things stand:**
▶ **Pillar Two:** domestic minimum top-up taxes apply from 2025 in the UAE, Bahrain, Qatar, Oman and Kuwait. Saudi Arabia has not implemented one yet.
▶ **E-invoicing:** live in Saudi Arabia, with the UAE from 2027 and other GCC states advancing their frameworks.
▶ **Personal income tax:** Oman has legislated a 5% tax on high earners from 1 January 2028, a first for the region.
▶ **Corporate tax:** Bahrain has proposed a general corporate tax, subject to legislative approval.
▶ **VAT:** amendments to the GCC Unified VAT Agreement strengthen cross-border coordination and information sharing.

The common thread is data and transparency. Authorities increasingly see transactions in real time and share information across borders.
