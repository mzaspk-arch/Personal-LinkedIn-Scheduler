**Three small policy gaps can block VAT recovery on staff benefits.**

FTA Decision No. 17 of 2026 sets out six types of free employee benefits where input VAT can be recovered, if they are provided under a contract or company policy and meet every condition.

The principle: business necessity supports recovery. Comfort, convenience and reward do not.

**Where it goes wrong:**
▶ **Transport with a cash option:** if staff can choose cash instead of the transport, the condition fails.
▶ **Temporary accommodation:** for new joiners it is limited to 30 days. A 40-day hotel stay goes beyond it.
▶ **Parking:** client-visit parking can qualify, but only with a documented policy and receipts kept.
▶ **Gym membership:** a reward, so not recoverable.

Each category has its own conditions, and all of them must be met. There is no single checklist.

✅ Compare your HR benefits policy with the six categories in the decision.

✅ Remove cash alternatives where you want to keep recovery, and keep the receipts.
