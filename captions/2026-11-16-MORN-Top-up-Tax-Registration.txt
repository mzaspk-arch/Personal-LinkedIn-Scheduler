**14 days left to register for UAE Top-up Tax.**

If your multinational group's first in-scope fiscal year ended before 30 April 2026, UAE entities that must register have to do so by 30 November 2026.

**Quick check:**
▶ **Scope:** groups with consolidated revenue of EUR 750 million or more in at least two of the last four fiscal years.
▶ **Deadline:** 30 November 2026 for fiscal years that ended before 30 April 2026. Otherwise, within seven months of the end of the first in-scope fiscal year.
▶ **Where:** on EmaraTax. This is separate from your Corporate Tax registration.
▶ **Zero tax still counts:** register even if you expect no Top-up Tax.
▶ **Penalty:** AED 10,000 per entity for late registration.

Remember that a mainly UAE group with one foreign subsidiary or branch can be in scope.
