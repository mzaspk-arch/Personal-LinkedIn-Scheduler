**A job title doesn't decide who is a connected person.**

Myth: only board members count as connected persons for UAE Corporate Tax.

The FTA's Public Clarification CTP010 (April 2026) looks at substance, not titles.

**Who counts (Article 36):**
▶ **Owners:** individuals who own or control the business, directly or indirectly.
▶ **Directors:** people on the board or an equivalent governing body. "Director" in a job title alone does not make someone one.
▶ **Officers:** anyone who plans and controls the business, makes strategic decisions, or can sign agreements that bind the company.
▶ **Their related parties.**

**Why it matters:** salaries and benefits paid to them are deductible only up to market value, and must be for the business. Listed companies and entities regulated by a UAE authority are excluded from this rule.

✅ Map who in your business can sign contracts or make strategic decisions, whatever their title.

✅ Keep evidence that their pay and benefits match the market.

Source: FTA Public Clarification CTP010: https://tax.gov.ae/Datafolder/Files/Pdf/2026/Guide/CTP010-Clarification-of-director-and-officer-04-2026.pdf
