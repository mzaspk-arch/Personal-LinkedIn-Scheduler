**"Odoo is free" is only half the story.**

A common belief among SME owners comparing ERPs: "Odoo is free, so our ERP will cost nothing."

Here is the full picture.

**Three facts:**
▶ **Two editions:** Odoo Community is open source. Odoo Enterprise is a paid subscription, priced per user.
▶ **Key apps sit in Enterprise:** apps many SMEs need, such as full Accounting, come with the Enterprise edition.
▶ **Licences are not the whole cost:** setup, data migration, training and support cost money on either edition.

None of this makes Odoo a bad choice. It means the budget should be honest from day one.

✅ List the apps you actually need, and check which edition includes each one.

✅ Ask for a quote that separates licences, implementation, data migration and training.
