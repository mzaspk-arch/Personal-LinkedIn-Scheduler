**Nature picks the method, never the obligation.**

Lesson 18 from Gharebon Ka MBA with Dr. Zeeshan-ul-Hassan Usmani.

Some gaps close with work: knowledge, practice, evidence, audience.

Temperament doesn't. The quiet person won't turn loud by trying harder.

**The 90-day test:**
▶ **Would deliberate practice visibly move it in three months?** It's a gap. Close it.
▶ **If not:** it's wiring. Build the work around it.

But that's a route, never an exemption.

The quiet person still has to sell, just in writing: long, careful, one message at a time. A written case survives being forwarded to whoever actually signs.

**Hear the difference:**
▶ **"I don't make cold calls"** is a strategy.
▶ **"I don't sell"** is a ceiling you chose yourself.

✅ Write down one thing you avoid, and test it against the 90-day question.

✅ If it's wiring, design a method that fits you, and keep the obligation.

Which one have you said: the strategy or the ceiling?
