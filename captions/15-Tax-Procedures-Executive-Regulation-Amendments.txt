**Found an error in a past return? 20 Business Days.**

Cabinet Decision No. 17 of 2026 amended the Executive Regulation of the Tax Procedures Law from 1 April 2026.

**What it means:**

▶ **Error above AED 10,000:** file a Voluntary Disclosure within 20 Business Days of becoming aware.
▶ **Error of AED 10,000 or less:** correct it in your next return.
▶ Overstated refund claims need a Voluntary Disclosure within 20 Business Days.
▶ Refund decisions are made within 20 Business Days, unless the FTA notifies you of a longer period.
▶ Refund still pending? Keep your records for an extra 2 years.

These rules apply across VAT, Excise and Corporate Tax.
