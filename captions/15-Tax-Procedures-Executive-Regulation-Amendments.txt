Found an error in a past return? 20 Business Days.

Cabinet Decision No. 17 of 2026 amended the Executive Regulation of the Tax Procedures Law from 1 April 2026.

What it means:

1. Error above AED 10,000: file a Voluntary Disclosure within 20 Business Days of becoming aware.

2. Error of AED 10,000 or less: correct it in your next return.

3. Overstated refund claims need a Voluntary Disclosure within 20 Business Days.

4. Refund decisions are made within 20 Business Days, unless the FTA notifies you of a longer period.

5. Refund still pending? Keep your records for an extra 2 years.

These rules apply across VAT, Excise and Corporate Tax.
