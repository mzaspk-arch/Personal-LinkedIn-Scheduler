**UAE Excise Tax has no registration threshold. Are you covered?**

The FTA's Excise Tax guide (January 2026) sets out the points importers and producers get wrong most often.

**Key compliance points:**
▶ **When tax applies:** on import, production, release from a Designated Zone, and certain stockpiling cases.
▶ **Registration:** no threshold. Notify the FTA within 30 days from the end of the month you start (or intend to start) taxable activities.
▶ **Sweetened drinks:** the 2026 update moves them to a tiered-volumetric model. The separate carbonated drinks category is removed and taxed under the model where applicable.
▶ **EmaraTax:** your declarations pre-populate your returns, so verify the numbers before you submit.
▶ **Returns and payment:** monthly, due by the 15th (next working day if it falls on a holiday or weekend).
▶ **Stockpiling:** "excess" stock, including more than 2 months' supply, can trigger liability. Keep audited stockpile reports.
▶ **Designated Zones:** tax suspension, but with strict controls, a Warehouse Keeper and annual renewals.
▶ **Digital Tax Stamps (DTS):** required on tobacco to support track-and-trace and enforcement.

✅ Reconcile your EmaraTax declarations with your stock records every month.

✅ Review your stock levels against the 2-month rule before each return.
