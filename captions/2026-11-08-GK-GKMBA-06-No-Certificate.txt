**A certificate is not on the list.**

Lesson 06 from Gharebon Ka MBA with Dr. Zeeshan-ul-Hassan Usmani.

Most courses end with a certificate. This one deliberately doesn't.

**What you walk out holding instead:**
▶ **A baseline ledger:** your income, hours, runway and the real rate you charge.
▶ **A positioning line:** one sentence a stranger repeats accurately.
▶ **A priced offer:** scope, tiers and a number you can say without flinching.
▶ **A 90-day plan:** the operating rhythm for the quarter after the course.

The programme is graded in receipts, not attendance.

As a CFO, this is the part I respect most. Outputs you can audit beat inputs you can frame.

The sessions are the easy part. The work happens between them: build something, put it where it can be judged, and ask someone for money or a decision.
