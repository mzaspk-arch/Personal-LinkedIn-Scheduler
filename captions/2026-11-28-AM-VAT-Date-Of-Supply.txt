**VAT can be due before the client pays.**

Many businesses wait for the customer's payment before declaring VAT.

The UAE VAT law does not work on cash received. It works on the date of supply.

**What the law says:**
▶ **Date of supply (Article 25):** the earliest of delivery of goods, completion of the service, receipt of payment or issue of the tax invoice.
▶ **Tax invoice (Article 67):** issue it within 14 days of the date of supply.
▶ **Periodic contracts (Article 26):** the earliest of the invoice date, the payment due date on the invoice, receipt of payment, or one year after the goods or services were provided.

So a service completed in November and paid in February usually belongs in the November period, not February.

Late customers do not delay the VAT you owe. Bad debt relief only applies after six months and a write-off.

✅ Match invoice dates to delivery or completion dates every month.

✅ Set your billing so invoices go out within 14 days of each supply.

Source: VAT Decree-Law Articles 25, 26 and 67 (tax.gov.ae).
