**Borrowing heavily? Your interest may be capped.**

UAE Corporate Tax does not always allow all your interest as a deduction.

The General Interest Deduction Limitation Rule applies to net interest expenditure: interest cost minus interest income.

**How it works:**
▶ **Up to AED 12 million:** net interest up to this amount in a 12-month period is not capped.
▶ **Above that:** you deduct the higher of AED 12 million or 30% of adjusted EBITDA (taxable income plus net interest, depreciation and amortisation).
▶ **Carry forward:** disallowed interest can be used in the next 10 tax periods, first in, first out.
▶ **Older debt:** interest on debt agreed before 9 December 2022 is outside the rule.

The AED 12 million is adjusted for tax periods longer or shorter than 12 months.

✅ Calculate net interest and 30% of adjusted EBITDA before you sign new financing.

✅ Track any disallowed interest so you can use it in later years.

Source: Corporate Tax Law Article 30, Ministerial Decision No. 126 of 2023 and FTA guide CTGIDL1 (tax.gov.ae).
