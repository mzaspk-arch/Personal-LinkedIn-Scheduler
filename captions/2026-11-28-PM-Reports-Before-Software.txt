**Choose software by the reports you need, not the demo.**

Software demos are built to impress. Your business runs on a few numbers.

Most SME owners buy on features, then find the reports they need are missing or need paid customisation.

**Three reasons to start with reports:**
▶ **Clarity:** list the numbers you check every week, such as cash, receivables, margins and stock, before you see any demo.
▶ **Proof:** ask the vendor to build those reports with a sample of your own data, not their demo data.
▶ **Cost:** features you never use still cost licences, training and support.

If a system cannot produce your weekly numbers simply, it will not be used, however good it looks.

✅ Write your top weekly reports on one page before you speak to any vendor.

✅ Make "show me these reports" the first item in every demo.
