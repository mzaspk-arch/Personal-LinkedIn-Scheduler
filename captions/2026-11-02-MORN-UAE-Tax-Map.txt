**Nine years, five tax regimes: the UAE tax map.**

**The milestones:**
▶ **January 2018:** VAT at 5%.
▶ **June 2023:** Corporate Tax at 9%, for financial years starting on or after 1 June 2023.
▶ **January 2025:** Domestic Minimum Top-up Tax at 15% for multinational groups with revenue of EUR 750 million or more.
▶ **January 2026:** R&D tax credit, with rates up to 50% on qualifying spend.
▶ **January 2027:** mandatory e-invoicing for businesses with revenue of AED 50 million or more. Smaller businesses follow on 1 July 2027.

The direction is clear: fewer grey areas, more data, and evidence before deductions.
