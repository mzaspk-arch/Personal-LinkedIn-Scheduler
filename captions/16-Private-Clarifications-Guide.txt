Unsure how a tax rule applies to you? Ask the FTA.

The FTA's updated Private Clarifications guide (TPGPC1) now covers VAT, Excise, Corporate Tax and Top-up Tax.

Key points:

1. Fee: AED 1,500 for one tax, AED 2,250 for more than one.

2. The FTA responds within 60 Business Days, longer for complex cases.

3. If the FTA asks for more information, you have 40 Business Days to reply.

4. Withdraw within 2 Business Days of submitting to get the fee back.

5. Only registered Tax Agents or Legal Representatives can apply for someone else.

A Private Clarification binds the FTA only for you, and only on the facts you gave.

✅ Check existing FTA guides and public clarifications first.

✅ Give complete facts. The answer is only as good as the request.
