**Claiming input VAT now needs proof you checked your supplier.**

FTA Decision No. 13 of 2026 took effect on 1 October 2026.

Before you deduct input VAT, you must verify the supplier and the transaction behind the invoice.

**What the FTA now expects:**
▶ **Supplier verification:** trade licence, authorised signatory ID and identity checks.
▶ **Business address checks:** confirm the supplier has a real place of business.
▶ **Risk assessment:** frequent address or staff changes, unusual volumes, other red flags.
▶ **Payment validation:** business reason, market price, payment method and who you paid.
▶ **Proper documentation:** a written policy, named owners and records of every check.

Small purchases under AED 10,000 (excluding VAT) are generally outside these checks, unless one supplier passes AED 100,000 in 12 months.

Skip the checks and your right to recover input VAT can be challenged.

✅ List your active suppliers and verify any not checked in the last 12 months.

✅ Write a short verification policy and name who owns it.
