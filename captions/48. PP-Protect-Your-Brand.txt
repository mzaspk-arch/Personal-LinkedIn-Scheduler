**Without registration, anyone can copy your brand.**

Your brand name, logo or slogan is your business identity.

In Pakistan, real legal protection starts when you register it as a trademark with IPO Pakistan.

**What registration gives you:**
▶ **Exclusive rights:** the name or logo is legally yours across Pakistan.
▶ **Protection:** you can stop others from copying your name or logo.
▶ **Credibility:** customers, partners and investors trust a registered brand.

**The process in four steps:**
▶ **Trademark search:** check that no one has registered something similar.
▶ **Application:** file with IPO Pakistan.
▶ **Examination and publication:** the mark is reviewed and published so others can object.
▶ **Registration certificate:** your mark is officially registered.

Protection lasts 10 years, and you can renew it for further 10-year periods.

Many small businesses only think about this after a copycat appears. By then, it is harder and costlier to fix.

✅ Run a trademark search before you print packaging or launch a website.

✅ File early, while the brand is still new.
