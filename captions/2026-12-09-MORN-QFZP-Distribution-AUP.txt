**Free Zone distributors now need an auditor's report to keep 0%.**

FTA Decision No. 6 of 2026 adds a new step for Qualifying Free Zone Persons that distribute goods in or from a Designated Zone. It applies to tax periods starting on or after 1 January 2026.

**What the report must confirm:**
▶ **Customer status:** customers buy the goods to resell them, or to process or alter them for sale.
▶ **Import route:** goods entering the UAE came in through a Designated Zone.

**How it works:**
▶ **Who:** an independent external auditor licensed in the UAE, under the agreed-upon procedures standard ISRS 4400.
▶ **Sample:** set by a formula in the decision, and it must include the highest-value transactions.
▶ **Deadline:** submit to the FTA no later than 30 days after the Corporate Tax return deadline.
▶ **Failure:** without the report, the distribution activity is treated as not meeting the qualifying conditions.

The decision applies only to the distribution activity, not to every qualifying activity.
