**Input VAT recovery now starts in procurement, not tax.**

Here is a common setup in a UAE business with hundreds of suppliers:

Procurement registers suppliers.
Accounts payable processes invoices and payments.
Tax prepares the VAT return.

Under FTA Decision No. 13 of 2026, input tax can be deducted only after the supplier and the supply have been verified.

So who owns the checks?

**My view:**
▶ **Procurement** should verify the supplier at onboarding, when documents are easiest to get.
▶ **Accounts payable** should confirm each supply: business reason, fair price, goods received and payment to the actual supplier.
▶ **Tax** should set the policy and review the evidence, not try to rebuild it months later.

If the evidence does not exist on the day of the deduction, it is very hard to create later.

✅ Write down who does each check, by name, not by department.

✅ Build the checks into your supplier onboarding form.

Agree or disagree? Who owns supplier verification in your business?
