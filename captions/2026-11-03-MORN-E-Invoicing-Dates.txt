**E-invoicing in the UAE has four go-live dates. Know yours.**

The rollout is phased by revenue, and the first wave goes live in two months.

**The dates:**
▶ **Revenue of AED 50 million or more:** the deadline to appoint an Accredited Service Provider (ASP) was 30 October 2026. Go-live is 1 January 2027.
▶ **Revenue below AED 50 million:** appoint an ASP by 31 March 2027. Go-live is 1 July 2027.
▶ **Government entities:** appoint an ASP by 31 March 2027. Go-live is 1 October 2027.
▶ **Transactions inside a VAT group:** excluded until 31 December 2028.
▶ **B2C sales:** outside the mandate for now.

If you are in the first wave and have not signed with an ASP yet, treat it as urgent and get advice on your position.

✅ Confirm your revenue band and your exact go-live date.
