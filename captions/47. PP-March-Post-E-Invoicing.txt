**E-invoicing becomes mandatory in the UAE from 1 January 2027.**

That is about two months away for the largest businesses.

A PDF sent by email will no longer count as a compliant tax invoice once your phase starts.

**The phased go-live dates:**
▶ **Revenue of AED 50 million or more:** 1 January 2027
▶ **Revenue below AED 50 million:** 1 July 2027, with an Accredited Service Provider appointed by 31 March 2027
▶ **Government entities:** 1 October 2027

**Getting ready means three things:**
▶ **Tax portal setup:** your registration details and the provider that will exchange invoices for you.
▶ **Digital reporting:** invoices issued in a structured format and reported to the FTA through that provider.
▶ **Regulatory alignment:** invoice fields, credit notes and approvals that match the new rules.

The hardest part is rarely the software.

It is clean customer master data, consistent tax codes and an ERP that can send the required fields.
