**Five stages of earning. Nobody skips one.**

Lesson 09 from Gharebon Ka MBA with Dr. Zeeshan-ul-Hassan Usmani.

**Locate yourself honestly:**
▶ **0 · Labour:** you sell hours. Stop working and income stops the same day.
▶ **1 · Skill:** you sell a specific ability. The rate rises, but hours still set the ceiling.
▶ **2 · Proof:** you sell an outcome. Buyers come by name, and price stops being the first question.
▶ **3 · System:** you sell what others deliver, such as a team, a product or a process.
▶ **4 · Capital:** ownership earns while you sleep.

Nobody skips a rung. But anyone can climb one inside 90 days.

The useful part of the exercise is the second sentence: write your stage number, then write why you're not one rung higher.

That sentence names your constraint before you've measured anything else.

✅ Write your stage number, with no flattery and no false modesty.

✅ Write why you're not one rung higher, and the date you plan to get there.

Which stage are you on today?
