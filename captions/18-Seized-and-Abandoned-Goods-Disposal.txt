**Goods seized? The FTA can auction them early.**

FTA Decision No. 7 of 2026 took effect on 30 July 2026.

It sets the procedure for selling seized and abandoned goods by public auction.

**Key points:**

▶ It covers goods that are perishable, at risk of shortage or leakage, or hazardous.
▶ Goods under investigation or in court proceedings need prior approval from the prosecution or court.
▶ The owner is notified before the sale.
▶ Proceeds pay costs first, then tax, then penalties. Any surplus goes to the owner.
▶ Waiting can cost you the goods, not just the tax.

✅ If your goods are seized, settle what's due quickly.

✅ Ask for the goods back in writing before an auction date is set.
