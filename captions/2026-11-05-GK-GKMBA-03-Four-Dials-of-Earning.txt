**Earning power has four dials.**

Lesson 03 from Gharebon Ka MBA with Dr. Zeeshan-ul-Hassan Usmani.

When income stalls, most people only reach for one dial: working harder.

**The four dials:**
▶ **Value:** solve costlier, more urgent problems than the ones filling your day.
▶ **Proof:** lower the buyer's risk with results, figures and references.
▶ **Reach:** let more of the right people find you and describe your work accurately.
▶ **Leverage:** stop trading hours alone. Borrow capacity from tools, media, teams or capital.

They reinforce each other. A little more proof makes reach work harder. A little more reach makes value easier to sell.

They don't multiply into a fantasy number either. Together, the aim is 3X.

✅ Score yourself 1 to 5 on each dial today.

✅ Pick the lowest one and give it one hour this week.

Which dial is lowest for you right now?
