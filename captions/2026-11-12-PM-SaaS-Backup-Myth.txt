**Your SaaS vendor is not your backup plan.**

"It's in the cloud, so our data is safe."

It is one of the most common beliefs among SME owners.

The cloud protects you from a server failure. It doesn't always protect you from your own team, or from your own contract.

**Who protects what:**
▶ **The vendor:** keeps the platform running and secure.
▶ **You:** decide who can edit, export and delete records. A user with the wrong rights can remove years of data in a click.
▶ **The contract:** says how long your data is kept after you cancel or stop paying. Many owners have never read that clause.

Backups only count if you can restore them.

✅ Schedule a monthly export of your key data: customers, invoices, stock and ledgers.

✅ Once a quarter, test that you can actually open and use that export.

Do you know how long your main software vendor keeps your data after you cancel?
