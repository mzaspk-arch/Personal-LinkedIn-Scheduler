**Spreadsheets aren't the problem. Unowned ones are.**

Most SMEs run part of the business on spreadsheets.

That isn't a failure. A spreadsheet is fast, flexible and cheap.

The trouble starts when nobody owns it.

**Why I'd fix ownership before buying software:**
▶ **No owner:** the person who built the formulas leaves, and nobody checks them again.
▶ **No single copy:** a file sent by email becomes five versions of the truth by Friday.
▶ **No priority:** not every sheet needs a system. Pricing, payroll and cash do.

Moving a messy spreadsheet into an ERP only gives you a messy ERP.

Give each critical sheet an owner, one shared location and a review date first. Then decide what to automate.

✅ List the spreadsheets your business could not run a week without.

✅ Put one name next to each, and move the top three into a proper system first.

Agree or disagree? Which spreadsheet would hurt most if it broke tomorrow?
