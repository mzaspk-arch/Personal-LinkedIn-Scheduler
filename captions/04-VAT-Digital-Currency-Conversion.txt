**Paid in crypto? Here's how the FTA wants it valued.**

Directive on Tax Transactions No. 3 of 2026 sets one method for VAT.

It covers supplies of digital currency and supplies paid for in digital currency.

**The method:**

▶ Choose 3 platforms from the FTA's list.
▶ Use the same 3 for the whole calendar year.
▶ Take each platform's rate at the date and time of supply or payment, as applicable.
▶ Convert to AED using the average of the 3 rates.
▶ Keep a record of each rate.

The platforms named: Binance FZE, Bybit Fintech FZE, Deribit FZE, Bitget and Payward FZCO.

Directives bind both the FTA and taxpayers.
