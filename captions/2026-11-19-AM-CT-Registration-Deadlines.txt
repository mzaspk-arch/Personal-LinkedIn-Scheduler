**Late Corporate Tax registration costs AED 10,000.**

The deadline is not the same for everyone. It depends on who you are and when you started.

**Know your deadline (FTA Decision No. 3 of 2024):**
▶ **New UAE or Free Zone company:** apply within 3 months of incorporation.
▶ **Freelancer or sole trader:** if your business turnover in 2026 exceeds AED 1 million, apply by 31 March 2027.
▶ **Foreign company managed and controlled in the UAE:** apply within 3 months of the end of its financial year.

The penalty for a late application is AED 10,000.

The deadline is for submitting the application, not for receiving your tax registration number.

Freelancers often miss this one, because turnover is counted across the whole calendar year.

✅ If you set up a company recently, check its incorporation date and apply now if you haven't.

✅ If you work for yourself, add up your 2026 business turnover in December so you know before 31 March.

Source: FTA Public Clarification on registration timelines: https://tax.gov.ae/en/media.centre/news/federal.tax.authority.issues.public.clarification.on.registration.timelines.for.taxable.persons.for.corporate.tax.aspx
