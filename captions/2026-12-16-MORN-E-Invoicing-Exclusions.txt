**Not every invoice has to be an e-invoice. Know the exclusions.**

The UAE e-invoicing mandate covers B2B and B2G supplies. Some transactions are excluded, which matters when you scope your project.

**Main exclusions:**
▶ **Government:** sovereign activities of government entities.
▶ **Airlines:** international passenger transport where an electronic ticket or electronic miscellaneous document is issued.
▶ **Financial services:** those exempt from VAT, and zero-rated financial services. Standard-rated financial services stay in scope.
▶ **Imported services** taxed under a foreign reverse charge.
▶ **Air cargo:** international transport of goods with an airway bill, excluded for 24 months.
▶ **B2C:** sales to consumers are outside the mandate for now.

**Still in scope:**
▶ Provisional or advance billing, which needs an e-invoice, with later changes made by credit note or a further e-invoice.
▶ Non-resident businesses registered for UAE VAT that must issue tax invoices.

Get the scope right first. Over-scoping wastes budget, and under-scoping creates penalties.
