**Unsure of the tax answer? Ask the FTA in writing.**

Some tax questions aren't answered by the law, the guides or the public clarifications.

For those, the FTA offers private clarifications through EmaraTax. Its guide, TPGPC1, was updated in July 2026.

**How it works:**
▶ **Check first:** read the law, FTA guides and public clarifications. The request must be about a real point of uncertainty.
▶ **Fee:** AED 1,500 for one tax, AED 2,250 if it covers more than one. It is generally not refundable.
▶ **What to upload:** a cover letter with the facts, your question, the law you considered, your own view and any alternative treatment, plus contracts, invoices and any advice you received.
▶ **Timing:** submit within 40 business days of starting the draft. The FTA aims to reply within 60 business days.
▶ **Language:** you get the answer in the language you ask in, English or Arabic.

Requests linked to a tax audit or assessment may be rejected.

✅ Write your own view and reasons first. It sharpens the question.

✅ Make sure the facts in your letter match your documents exactly.

Source: FTA Private Tax Clarifications service: https://tax.gov.ae/en/services/tax.clarifications.request.aspx
