**Client dinners are only 50% deductible.**

A common Corporate Tax mistake: treating client entertainment like any other business cost.

Article 32 of the UAE Corporate Tax Law allows only 50% of entertainment, amusement or recreation expenditure as a deduction.

**What it covers:**
▶ **Who:** entertaining customers, shareholders, suppliers and other business partners.
▶ **What:** meals, accommodation, transportation, admission fees, and facilities or equipment used for that entertainment.
▶ **Condition:** the cost must still be incurred wholly and exclusively for your business under Article 28.

So AED 100,000 of client hospitality gives a deduction of AED 50,000. The other half is added back in your Corporate Tax return.

The problem is rarely the rule. It is the bookkeeping: entertainment mixed into travel, marketing or staff costs.

✅ Open a separate ledger account for client entertainment.

✅ Record who was entertained and why on every receipt.

Source: Federal Decree-Law No. 47 of 2022, Article 32 (tax.gov.ae).
