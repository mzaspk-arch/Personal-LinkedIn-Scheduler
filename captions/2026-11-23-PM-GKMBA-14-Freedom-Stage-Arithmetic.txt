**Your salary doesn't set your freedom stage.**

Lesson 14 from Gharebon Ka MBA with Dr. Zeeshan-ul-Hassan Usmani.

A surgeon on a large salary can be on stage one. A shopkeeper with rent coming in can be on stage four.

**Six stages of financial freedom:**
▶ **0 · Dependent:** someone else covers your costs.
▶ **1 · Survival:** you earn, but one illness or lost job ends it.
▶ **2 · Cushion:** three months of expenses saved, no interest-charging debt.
▶ **3 · Security:** unworked income covers your essentials.
▶ **4 · Independence:** it covers the life you live now.
▶ **5 · Freedom:** it covers the life you want, and work becomes a choice.

**Two divisions locate you:**
▶ **Runway:** savings ÷ monthly essentials, in months.
▶ **Coverage:** unworked income ÷ monthly essentials, as a percentage.

Under one month of runway is stage one, whatever the salary says.

✅ Calculate your runway in months tonight.

✅ Then your coverage percentage, and write down your stage.

What would move you up one stage this year?
