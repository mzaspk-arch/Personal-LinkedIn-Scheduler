**Before you sign a SaaS contract, read the exit terms.**

Most SME owners compare features and price per user.

Few read what happens when they want to leave, or when the price changes. That is where the surprises are.

**Four checks before you sign:**
▶ **Data export:** in what format can you export your data, how often, and does it cost extra?
▶ **Renewal price:** is there a cap on how much the price can rise each year?
▶ **Auto-renewal:** how many days' notice do you need to give to cancel?
▶ **Data location:** where is your data hosted, and does that matter for your clients or regulators?

A cheap first year can turn into an expensive lock-in if these answers are missing.

✅ Ask the vendor to answer these four points in writing before you sign.

✅ Put the cancellation notice date in your calendar the day the contract starts.
