**Subsidiaries incorporated abroad can also be exempt from UAE Corporate Tax.**

Cabinet Decision No. 55 of 2025 extended the exemption for subsidiaries of Exempt Persons to juridical persons incorporated outside the UAE. It applies retrospectively from 1 June 2023.

**The conditions:**
▶ **Ownership:** the entity is wholly owned and controlled by an Exempt Person, such as a government entity, a government-controlled entity, a qualifying investment fund or a qualifying pension fund.
▶ **Activity:** it exclusively does the exempt owner's business in whole or in part, exclusively holds assets or invests funds for it, or carries out activities ancillary to it.
▶ **Application:** the exemption is claimed by applying to the FTA.

**Who should look again:**
Mainly foreign-incorporated entities managed and controlled in the UAE, which are therefore UAE tax residents. Some were registered and taxed only because they were incorporated abroad.

Because the decision is retrospective, earlier registrations and returns may need to be revisited.
