**Banks: AT1 coupons outside P&L are not deductible.**

That is the FTA's position in Public Clarification CTP012.

**The reasoning:**

▶ Taxable Income starts from Accounting Income, the net profit or loss in the financial statements (Article 20).
▶ Payments on Additional Tier 1 instruments that are not included in Accounting Income are not deductible.
▶ "Payments" means coupons or dividends, not repayment of principal.
▶ AT1 instruments classed as equity under IFRS typically pay coupons through equity, not profit or loss.
▶ How AT1 holders are taxed is outside this clarification.

The outcome follows the accounting treatment.

✅ Model the tax impact of equity versus liability treatment.
