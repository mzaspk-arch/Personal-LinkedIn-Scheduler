**Your 2027 budget should start with cash, not profit.**

Budget season is here, and most SME budgets start with a sales target and a profit number.

I think that is the wrong starting point.

**Three reasons:**
▶ **Profit counts sales you haven't collected yet.** Cash pays salaries, rent and suppliers.
▶ **Tax dates are fixed.** VAT and Corporate Tax payments leave your account on set dates, whether customers have paid or not.
▶ **A monthly cash forecast shows the gap before your bank does.** You see a tight month early enough to act.

A profitable business can still run out of cash. A cash-led budget makes that visible.

✅ Build your 2027 budget month by month, with expected collections, not just sales.

✅ Put your VAT return dates and Corporate Tax payment date into the cash plan from day one.

Agree or disagree? Does your budget start with profit or with cash?
