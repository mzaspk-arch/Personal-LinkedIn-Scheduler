**Renting out property? Know the tax year 2027 slabs.**

Rental income is taxed on the gross annual rent, not the profit after expenses.

For individuals and AOPs, FBR's Withholding Tax Rate Card for tax year 2027 (section 155) sets these slabs:

**Gross annual rent:**
▶ **Up to Rs 300,000:** nil.
▶ **Rs 300,000 to 600,000:** 5% of the amount above Rs 300,000.
▶ **Rs 600,000 to 2 million:** Rs 15,000 plus 10% of the amount above Rs 600,000.
▶ **Above Rs 2 million:** Rs 155,000 plus 25% of the amount above Rs 2 million.

For companies the rate is 15% on the ATL and 30% off it.

Example: annual rent of Rs 1.2 million means Rs 15,000 plus 10% of Rs 600,000, so Rs 75,000.

✅ Work out your annual tax on rent now, before the year ends.

✅ Keep tenancy agreements and any withholding certificates for your return.

Source: FBR Withholding Tax Rate Card, Tax Year 2027 (fbr.gov.pk).
