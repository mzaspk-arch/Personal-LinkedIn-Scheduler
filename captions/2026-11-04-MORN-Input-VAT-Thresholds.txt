**Three numbers now decide whether you can claim input VAT.**

Since 1 October 2026, FTA Decision No. 13 requires you to verify the supplier and the supply before you deduct input tax.

A valid tax invoice on its own is no longer enough.

**The thresholds (excluding VAT):**
▶ **AED 10,000:** individual supplies below this may skip the checks.
▶ **AED 100,000:** if supplies from one supplier exceed this in 12 months, or are expected to, the small-supply exception no longer applies.
▶ **AED 375,000:** above this, add enhanced checks, including the supplier's UAE bank account and a review of public information.

Checks are needed when you first engage a supplier, and again where it has not been verified in the previous 12 months.
