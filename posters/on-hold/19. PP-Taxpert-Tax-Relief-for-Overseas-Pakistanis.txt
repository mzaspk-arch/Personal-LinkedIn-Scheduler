**Overseas Pakistanis: non-filer? You can still pay filer rates.**

Good news if you live abroad and buy or sell property back home.

If you hold a POC or NICOP and are non-resident, you can now pay filer tax rates on property deals.

This applies even if you are a non-filer.

**Who qualifies:**
▶ **Card:** you hold a POC or NICOP.
▶ **Residency:** you stay in Pakistan for less than 183 days in a financial year.
▶ **Deal:** you are buying or selling property in Pakistan.

**How it works:**
▶ Apply through the FBR Overseas Pakistanis portal.
▶ Upload your documents.
▶ Once approved, you pay tax at the reduced filer rates under Sections 236C and 236K.

✅ Count your days in Pakistan for the year before you sign.

✅ Keep a scanned copy of your POC or NICOP ready for the upload.
