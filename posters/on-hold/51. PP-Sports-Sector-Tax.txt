**Some UAE sports bodies can be fully exempt from Corporate Tax.**

Cabinet Decision No. 1 of 2026 exempts certain non-commercial sports entities, with effect back to 1 June 2023.

It covers sports entities, international sports entities and their ancillary entities that promote, manage and develop sport at regional and international levels.

**The conditions:**
▶ **Purpose:** no business activity except what directly serves the entity's sports objectives.
▶ **Use of funds:** income and assets used only for those objectives and reasonable related costs.
▶ **No private benefit:** no income or assets available to members, founders or shareholders personally.
▶ **Transparency:** data must be provided to the FTA on request.

Fail a condition and the exemption is lost from the start of that tax period.

The aim is to support the growth of the sector and attract global sports organisations to the UAE.
