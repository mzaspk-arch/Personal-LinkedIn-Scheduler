# Personal LinkedIn Scheduler

Posters and captions for Zubair Akbar's personal LinkedIn profile, published automatically by a Claude scheduled task.

- `schedule.json` — one entry per post: `date` (Dubai time), `num`, `image`, `caption`.
- `posters/NN.png` — 1080 × 1350 poster for post NN.

Schedule: Monday to Saturday at about 11:00 AM (Asia/Dubai). No posts on Sunday.

To add more posts: add the PNG to `posters/` and a new entry to `schedule.json` with its date and caption.
